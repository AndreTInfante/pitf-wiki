    Quotes¶

    This page collects the best lines of PITF, grouped by arc in story order and then by episode. Each entry names the player, the character they are speaking as, and the moment. Lines marked "out of character" were said by the player at the table and not by a character. Every non-player character is voiced by Andre, the game master.

    An ellipsis marks a place where a line has been trimmed or where someone else at the table cut in. The unrecorded sessions have no quotes. For the jokes that recur across episodes, see Running gags.

    Hall of fame¶

    • Andre (as Rutherford): "It's like a roller coaster, except you talk to God." (Radford Horror 04)
    • Ezra (as The Biz): "I now believe in aliens, and I hate them." Joining the fight against the moth. (Radford Horror 07)
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Moth to a flame, bitch!" Setting the monster on fire. (Radford Horror 07)
    • Zack (as Huell Kendrick): "Hey Howard, you've been shot, buddy. Welcome to the team." At Andrew's hospital bed. (Radford Horror 08)
    • Andre (as Briggs): "I think every damned soul becomes a seagull. That's my little heresy on top of Catholicism." A fisherman's theology. (Summer Service 01)
    • Andre (as Gray-skinned dream visitor): "Are you high enough in your government to order a nuclear strike? That might work." Advice on the thing in the water. (Summer Service 03)
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "All right, boys, raise your hand. Who wants to cause a nuclear incident?" (Summer Service 06)
    • Andre (as Donald McManley): "What do we do if the universe turns out to just be full of the stupidest fucking thing I have ever heard in my life?" An existential rant at the office. (Appalachia 01)
    • Zack (as Huell Kendrick): "Sir, I am an officer. Do not touch me. I am listening to the menu." In line at the Spud Hut. (Appalachia 02)
    • Rita (as Andrew Howard): "We're not surveilling Bill Clinton. We're surveilling dead Chewbacca." Pleading innocence to Big Bill. (Appalachia 07)
    • Dante (out of character): "Apes mine gold... Tell nobody, 'cause ape." The whole operation, recapped for Zack. (Appalachia 09)
    • Andre (as George H. W. Bush): "There is coincidence, there is miracle, and there is enemy action." His theory of the Georgia events. (Walkers 01)
    • Andre (as Ponytail Lady): "The number two most important rule of living here is to keep the fucking goose happy." House rules at the ranch. (Walkers 02)
    • Andre (as Donald McManley): "This is not a person. This is a vehicle. Somebody made this. And whatever you killed inside of it was its pilot." His verdict on a Skinwalker body. (Walkers 08)
    • Rita (as Andrew Howard): "Did you call the right person? Like, there are a lot of Andrew Howards in the world." To the President of the United States. (Roulet Estate 03)
    • Andre (as Crash survivor): "Not aliens. The opposite of aliens." Answering Yeltsin. (Radford Horrors 04)

    The Radford Horror¶

    Radford Horror 01¶

    • Andre (as Mr. King): "Jesus and the Buddha were not wholly of this world, and neither are you. Remember that." Parting words to Andrew in the flashback.
    • Rita (as Andrew Howard): "But that ain't mean. That's just... that's just rad." On learning his nickname is "Andrew the Alien."
    • Andre (as Kurt's boss): "What I needed was a German Shepherd and what I got was a crocodile." Downsizing Kurt.
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "You know none of the green men stuff has panned out so far, right?" Accepting the assignment.
    • Rita (as Andrew Howard): "We're gonna, you know, travel around, find monsters and shit." Briefing Dick and Kurt.
    • Ezra (as The Biz): "We get dental. This is about to be the bleeding edge for my liver." Reply to "bleeding edge."
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Not your average spook. I think of myself as more of a Casper." To Deputy Underhill.
    • Andre (as Rutherford): "When they're sending UFOs, they aren't sending their best." The alien debate at the Pay n' Stay desk.
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Those things will stand for a thousand years, I swear." About a World Trade Center postcard.
    • Andre (as Tyler): "I want to be a spaceman." When told he could grow up to be an agent.
    • Andre (as Sheriff Frank Roosevelt): "I just want to make sure there isn't gonna be any... cowboy bullshit going on." Setting ground rules.
    • Andre (as Diane): "My life has gotten a lot more surreal since about 6 this morning." After spotting a landmine.

    Radford Horror 02¶

    • Ezra (as The Biz): "Sir, I work for the FBI. It is. Hundred percent." Response to the father calling Usenet "probably perverts."
    • Andre (as Sheriff Frank Roosevelt): "It's not fishing. It's perverts. You don't go fishing for perverts." Reaction to the bait-dog idea.
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Using an animal in an op almost never works out. They're unpredictable, they're unprofessional." On the dog bait plan.
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Trust me when I say that these kinda perverts you're looking for are very particular about their beans." The coffee shop theory.
    • Zack (as Huell Kendrick): "Supposing I wanted to fraudulently fill a prescription, how might I go about doing that?" To the pharmacist.
    • Andre (as Sheriff Frank Roosevelt): "Listen, take it up with the taxpayers. People do not like their taxes around here." On funding for deputies.
    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Come back in honor or not at all, American pig dog." What Kurt's wife shouted down the phone.
    • Zack (out of character): "Two spacemen, what are the odds?" On the sheriff missing the link.
    • Andre (out of character): "Gotta love a D&D campaign with homework." As the players compare case notes.
    • Andre (as GM): "I think the greatest monster is man." On the waiting room.
    • Dante (out of character): "I've Tabasco-boarded men for less." On pressuring Lori.

    • Dante (as Kurt Larson): "Parking like an asshole is the same as probable cause, right?" At the Augustine house.

    • Andre (as GM): "Failed heist. Yeah, calling it a heist is generous... the Ocean's Eleven montage would be sad." After the clinic attempt.
