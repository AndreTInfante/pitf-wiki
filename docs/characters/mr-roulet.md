# Mr. Roulet

**Mr. Roulet** is the never-named husband of [Margaret Roulet](margaret-roulet.md), a gun collector who owned the Rhode Island house the task force investigates.

## Description and background

In [Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md) Andrew learns from the library that the house belonged to a husband and wife, and that the wife was the more devout UFO believer. Wedding photos from the 1950s or 60s, apparently taken in Arizona, show a curly-haired woman and a man with close-cropped military hair. The house has a combination-locked steel door hiding a [weapons closet](../locations/weapons-closet.md) whose empty racks once held about twenty firearms; only a World War II flamethrower was left behind. The man is never given a first name. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*

In [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md) the agents find guns that look like they came from his closet in the [Hidden Attic Lair](../locations/hidden-attic-lair.md), including a Kalashnikov and Russian World War II-era pistols, many of them loaded. Andre notes that "the gentleman of the house was a firearm collector", and the agents reason that this explains how the intruder came by a Soviet [VSS rifle](../items/vss-rifle.md) and makes a KGB plot less likely. *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

## Fate

Parts of two bodies are found in the [Roulet Basement](../locations/roulet-basement.md) trash pit. Margaret is identified by her ring; the second body is never identified, though the agents assume it is her husband. The attic lair also held older Polaroids of other, unidentified sleepers. *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

At the pharmacy siege, a police officer reports that the gunman has a machine gun, a flamethrower and an old wooden rifle, and the intruder is later seen carrying a Mosin-Nagant. The episodes do not say whether these came from the Roulet collection. See [The Roulet Intruder](../monsters/roulet-intruder.md). *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
