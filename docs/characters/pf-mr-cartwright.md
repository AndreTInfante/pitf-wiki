# Mr. Cartwright

!!! note "Non-canon"
    Mr. Cartwright belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Mr. Cartwright** is Colonel Sparago's clipboard aide, who explains why Ovain broke down and shrugs off Mirabeth's slap. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Description

He is a young or middle-aged man with a receding hairline and big wire-framed glasses, who slinks out of the crowd with a clipboard when Sparago calls for him. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

Andre first called him Mr. Briggs, then asked the table to name him and renamed him Mr. Cartwright on the spot. After [Ovain](pf-ovain.md)'s outburst, he explains that Ovain's handlers put him on online dating as part of his treatment and that it has gone badly ("millionaire famous athlete, like seven and a half feet tall, kind of a no-brainer, right?"). He adds that Ovain "may have been doing some journaling recently about killing the president again." *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

[Mirabeth](pf-mirabeth.md) slaps at him twice in quick succession. Her Directly Engage a Threat roll is a 4, so he simply ignores her and she marks Insecure. He answers that it is a "shockingly tough dating market out there." *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
