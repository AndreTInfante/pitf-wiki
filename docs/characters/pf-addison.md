# Addison

!!! note "Non-canon"
    Addison belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Addison** is an exhausted, poor mother at the pre-event social who pays [Mirabeth](pf-mirabeth.md) a $400 down payment on an invented essential-oil business. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Description

Addison hovers over her teenage son while he works on his flag for the children's event, critiquing every line he draws. The pair look very poor, and a pile of abandoned flags sits beside them. At the social ([The Flag Contest](../lore/pf-flag-contest.md)), Mirabeth uses Assess the Situation to find the person least willing to attend, and Andre describes Addison and her son as the weak point she senses. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

Mirabeth lures Addison away on the pretext of a chat while [Derk](pf-derk.md) talks to her son. She improvises a pitch about "the oil pyramid" (see [The Oil Pyramid](../items/pf-oil-pyramid-kit.md)), telling Addison that a parent's financial stability is what steadies a child's life. Mirabeth rolls a 10 on Provoke Someone, and Addison pays $400 down. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

Her son then runs over sobbing, "Mommy, I don't wanna get killed with the president," after Derk has told him that [Ovain](pf-ovain.md) is coming to kill the president. Addison asks whether there is a threat, hears that the tournament has been suspended after an incident with a competitor, and says she needs to make some calls. She hurries her son to the car, promising to call Mirabeth about the oils. Mirabeth gives her a business card. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
