# Harrison

**Harrison** is a stoned, apologetic Ballard high schooler caught hiding in the Sherman Ranch barn on a dare, who tells the team about the stench at his school.

## Description

Harrison is polite, apologetic and bad at crime. He wears a holographic snap bracelet, carries a backpack of rolling papers, abysmal weed, textbooks and dog-eared *Goosebumps* books (*Welcome to Dead House*, *Monster Blood*, *Stay Out of the Basement*), and wants to impress a girl *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*.

## History

While the team guards the guest house against the goose, [the Biz](dick-baxter.md) spots a glint near the barn. Kurt and Huell enter and, with a Read a Bad Situation roll (9), find two extremely stoned teenagers hiding behind equipment; the glint was the snap bracelet *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*.

The party plays rough FBI ("decontamination protocol," a flashlight in the eyes). [Walt Murphy](walt-murphy.md) recognizes Harrison, whom he has not seen since the boy was about seven. Harrison explains the dare: spend an hour on the ranch looking for ghosts or aliens. He says his school has about 60 students from the whole county and an eye-watering smell this semester that students blame on sewage or a dead thing in the air conditioning. Kurt connects the smell to the town's odd-tasting water. He also describes the school's "virginity anthem," sung to the tune of "We Are the Champions"; the Biz makes him sing part of it at gunpoint until Rita stops it *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*. See [Ballard High School](../locations/ballard-high-school.md) and [Ballard water contamination](../lore/ballard-water-contamination.md).

His silent friend gives his name as Marshall Crichton and will not say why he came. Their weed comes from Chuck, a friend of Harrison's brother, for $25. At the guest house Kurt takes their weed and returns good weed; a Manipulate Someone roll (10) frightens Harrison into taking it. The boys bike off from the front gate *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*.
