# Mirabeth's Bridge-Club Friend

!!! note "Non-canon"
    This character belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Mirabeth's Bridge-Club Friend** is the harried, unnamed organizer of the president's children's event, who arguably owes [Mirabeth](pf-mirabeth.md) a favor. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Description

A harried, middle-aged woman who answers the video phone in the middle of organizing the event for the president. She knows [Derk](pf-derk.md) as "that poor boy with the horse feed," and her first guess when Mirabeth calls is "Oh god, are you in jail?" *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

After the tournament briefing, Mirabeth learns that the national-holiday logo event for disadvantaged children ([The Flag Contest](../lore/pf-flag-contest.md)) is being run partly by an old bridge buddy on the committee. Mirabeth calls her from a street-corner video phone outside [Miguel](pf-miguel.md)'s stand, while Derk's noisy lunch goes on behind her ("it's like Animal Planet!"). *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

The friend says the age limit is fifteen, and Mirabeth claims Derk is fourteen. Mirabeth's Pierce the Mask roll is a 3, so the friend offers only a place on the wait list and, when Mirabeth asks about friends for Derk, the address of a social in [the Capital](../locations/pf-capital.md) a couple of hours away. Late that night she phones to say a spot has opened. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
