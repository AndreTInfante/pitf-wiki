# Tyler

**Tyler** is a four-year-old boy from the trailer across the street from the Lucky house in Radford, Virginia, who saw a "spaceman" above the missing girl's home.

## Description

Tyler is an insomniac four-year-old in Pooh Bear pajamas, miserable at being woken. He knows [Amanda Lucky](../characters/amanda-lucky.md) only from trick-or-treating, has seen spacemen only on TV, and wants to be one when he grows up. His mother Mary, named once by Sheriff [Frank Roosevelt](../characters/frank-roosevelt.md), is reluctant to wake him ("he had a dream") and provides orange Tang. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*

## History

On the task force's first morning investigating Amanda Lucky's disappearance, the team crosses the street from the [Lucky house](../locations/lucky-house.md), about 100 feet away. [Dick Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md) gives Tyler an FBI sticker, and [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md) gives him a "Viet Cong tooth" and tells him they are secret agents. Tyler says he saw a light and a spaceman all in white with a helmet, up above the Lucky house, who "just went down" without obviously falling or using a ladder, and who was gone when Tyler came back from telling his parents. Asked by Kurt, he insists: "He was a spaceman. He looked like a spaceman." Told he could grow up to be an agent, he answers: "I want to be a spaceman." *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*

[Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) speaks to Tyler privately while his parents are out of the room. Sheriff Roosevelt does not think he saw a spaceman and says he "had a dream." [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) proposes a man in a motorcycle suit and helmet, and privately a person in a hazmat suit; the Biz also suspects a sleepy child mistook a motorcyclist. Nothing is confirmed in the episode. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*

The figure later becomes known as [The Spaceman](../monsters/spaceman.md), eventually tied to Abelforth Whateley's homemade spacesuit and then to [Wilbur Whateley](../characters/wilbur-whateley.md); see [Spaceman suit](../items/spaceman-suit.md). Tyler does not appear again.
