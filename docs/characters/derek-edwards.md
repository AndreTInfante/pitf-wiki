# Derek Edwards

**Derek Edwards** is a tall, bearded amateur photographer and contractor for Auraria's small newspaper, the *Gold Bug*, whose Chewbacca costume was stolen from Apollo's dry cleaners and later turned up on a dead man.

## Description

Derek is quite tall, with a very long beard, a ponytail and a vest. He has no military record. He uses computers a great deal and runs the newspaper's website, and he puts out hunting-blind cameras to photograph deer. He is cooperative *([Appalachia 06](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/06.md))*.

## History

After the team finds a dead man in a Chewbacca costume on [Frotter Comstock](frotter-comstock.md)'s land, Kurt goes to the *Gold Bug* to find the source of the newspaper's Bigfoot photo. A pinched older woman at the shopfront names the photographer as "Derek Richter", and Andre immediately corrects it to Derek Edwards. She says he is a contractor rather than staff, and volunteers that she believes he is gay, which prompts Andrew to point out that gay people can have friends. She fetches a number from a dusty Rolodex *([Appalachia 06](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/06.md))*.

A young-sounding man who gives his name as Carlos, and who says he is a friend of Mr. Edwards, answers the phone and hands it to an older-sounding Derek. Derek says the photos came from a motion-sensor camera, so nobody helped him. When Kurt mentions the Chewbacca costume, Derek says his own was stolen: he sent it to be dry cleaned eight months earlier and it vanished from the rack. He had bought it for cash at Comic-Con, with no receipt, and says it smelled strongly like "fat nerd", which is why he was having it cleaned. The cleaners was Apollo's, down on Second *([Appalachia 06](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/06.md))*.

At Apollo's, the man at the front desk confirms the theft. The latch on the double door of the storage area had been broken for months, and he paid Derek $100 for the loss. He recognizes the photo of the dead man as an out-of-town drifter who stood around watching, sometimes with a walkie-talkie, and who he thinks stayed at the [Motel and Suites](../locations/motel-and-suites.md). The lead points the team toward the dead man, later identified as [Troy Cleaver](troy-cleaver.md), and not toward Derek *([Appalachia 06](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/06.md))*.
