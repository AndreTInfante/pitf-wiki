# Henry

**Henry** is [Donald McManley](donald-mcmanley.md)'s "useless" assistant, sent to Salem by Vespa to fetch ivermectin, who calls in with a gore-covered umbrella having "killed something."

## Description

Henry is confused by the request for "worm medicine" and has no rain gear. He rides a Vespa *([Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md))*.

## History

### The Summer Service

Donald McManley is reluctant ("you guys can just drive to Salem"), but a Manipulate Someone roll of 13, made with the Biz as the team's most charming member, gets him to lend his assistant: "You can take Henry. He's useless anyway." The team sends Henry to Salem for ivermectin and any broad-spectrum antiparasitic, with a concealed carry permit as makeshift FBI cover. He asks for an umbrella (none to spare) and leaves into the storm *([Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md))*. See [Ivermectin](../items/ivermectin.md).

After the hotel siege, a muffled signal from the Salem direction proves to be Henry (Andre): "I just killed a guy, I think. Or something." The team finds him on a Vespa with a gore-covered umbrella, the scooter soaked in blood and viscera. He hands over a pharmacy bag with the ivermectin ("Here's your worm pills. I'm going to bed"), shoves the Vespa off the road and climbs into the cramped vehicle: "I hate it here. It sucks so bad." His last line closes the session *([Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*.

## Unresolved

The episodes do not say what he killed near Salem; Dante (as [Kurt Larson](kurt-larson.md)) guesses it was an infected person, "if they exploded."
