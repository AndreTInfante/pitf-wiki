# Hooster

**Hooster** is the Biz's confidential informant in Washington, D.C., a "criminal MacGyver" on parole who agrees to build a bomb collar, supply thermite and sell a net gun to deal with the Sherman Ranch goose.

## Description

Hooster is a D.C. informant out on parole and working shifts at Arby's. His wife is pregnant again (conjugal visits). He has elaborate crime tools, including an electromagnet for store windows and movie-style traps *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*. The Biz arrested him, and Hooster says: "You're the nicest guy I've ever been arrested by." Hooster sent the Biz dirty magazines in jail *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*.

## History

After the goose steals Howard's jacket and torments the team, the Biz decides he wants a bomb collar to "give the goose as a peace offering." He uses the Fixer move (Charm, result 12) to reach Hooster by phone. Hooster agrees to make a bomb collar with minimal collateral damage and absolutely no dogs, off the books and for cash; to supply thermite; and to sell a net gun as an anti-goose measure. He refuses to make fake FBI badges. He will mail the package in a flat-rate envelope after his Arby's shift *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*. See [Nancy the goose](nancy-the-goose.md).

His price is a policeman's ball invitation for him and his wife, plus help with D.C. parking tickets, which the Biz accepts. Howard excuses himself to the bathroom to avoid the call. Huell's request for a revolver-shotgun hybrid is forgotten before the call ends. The package is due in a few days *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*.
