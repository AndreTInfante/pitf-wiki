# Miguel

!!! note "Non-canon"
    Miguel belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Miguel** is the honest vendor of a hot dog taco stand who gives Derk 98 hot dogs and $2 change for a $100 bill. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Description

Miguel runs a street stand about a quarter mile outside the athletes' compound. He sells a hot dog in a taco shell with spicy cheese, remarkably fresh tomatoes and pico de gallo, at a dollar apiece. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

[Derk](pf-derk.md) and [Mirabeth](pf-mirabeth.md) land beside the stand after the wall jump, catching Miguel mid-drop with a hot dog in his tongs. He greets them with "Hola, señor" and a stiff welcome. Mirabeth gives Derk the cash to hand over, and Derk passes Miguel a hundred-dollar bill. Miguel, who is honest, empties the cart to pile up 98 hot dogs and returns $2. Derk tries to give the $2 back for more, but Miguel refuses ("Sir, sir, no, no more, no") and wheels his cart away, dry-heaving as Derk eats them all. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

The $2 later pays for Derk's video call from [Wisukonshin](../locations/pf-wisukonshin.md).
