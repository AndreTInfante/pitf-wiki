# Older Guard officer

**The older Guard officer** is an unnamed National Guard officer in command of the checkpoint at the entrance to Marblehead, who debriefs the task force at the end of *The Summer Service*.

## Description

Andre describes him as an older gentleman in a lieutenant's uniform. A young lieutenant at the checkpoint first turns the team away, saying that nobody may enter or leave after "a biological incident" and offering to divert them to a hotel set up outside town. When Kurt says they are the FBI agents the Guard has been looking for, the young lieutenant takes them to the older officer *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*. His name is never given.

## History

The officer tells the team he has been told they have a canister of liquid that they call worm cure. [Kurt Larson](kurt-larson.md) says it should kill the worms but not human tissue and hands over the half-empty [anti-worm agent canister](../items/anti-worm-agent-canister.md), which the officer sends to the chemist. Kurt explains that the team has been intercepting a splinter group of bioterrorists who tried to cause an incident at the nuclear plant. The officer calls it "a clusterfuck" and sends them to a tent with a phone *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.

After the team's call to [Big Bill](big-bill.md), in which the two agree to tell everyone that terrorists were responsible, the officer asks [Andrew Howard](andrew-howard.md) for his account. He mentions the "real weird murals" in City Hall and says the group failed to take down the plant but killed a few of the guards. Andrew cites the group's literature about returning to the ocean and describes a parasite put into much of the local food supply; Kurt and Andrew say that the origin of the canister and any link to the [synthetic nerve agent](../lore/synthetic-nerve-agent.md) are unclear. The officer replies that his field medics are reporting "profoundly wild things," that the military is distributing deworming medicine, and that some patients will not pull through. See [Terrorist cover story](../lore/terrorist-cover-story.md) *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.

He sends the team to the medical tent for antivirals and hot soup. As he leaves to speak to his superior "about worms," he mentions that "someone used a tactical nuke at the nuclear power plant" and that it will be hard to keep out of the news. The team feigns surprise ("That scoundrel. What?"); the weapon in question is the team's own [M28 Davy Crockett](../items/davy-crockett.md) *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.
