# Fatty Gibbs

!!! note "Non-canon"
    Fatty Gibbs belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Fatty Gibbs** was a very sweet sumo wrestler and Derk's friend, who died of a coronary artery problem earlier last year. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Description

Andre describes Fatty as a morbidly obese, very strong sumo wrestler and a proficient regional fighter, though not world-class, and "just the nicest person that you have ever met in your life." He made friends with [Derk](pf-derk.md) shortly after Derk landed on Earth, showed him how to buy food other than horse feed, and gave him his old couch. He was almost the only person who was kind to Derk before Derk met [Mirabeth](pf-mirabeth.md). *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

Fatty died earlier last year. Derk agreed to enter the World Martial Arts Tournament because its grand prize, the [Philosopher's Stone](../items/pf-philosophers-stone.md), can be used to resurrect him. The tournament is suspended during the one-shot, which ends with this goal unfulfilled. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
