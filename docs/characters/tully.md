# Talila "Tully"

**Talila "Tully"** is a seventeen-year-old front-desk clerk at the Smith Hotel in Dahlonega, Georgia, who overhears Andrew Howard blurting out the case's body count to Big Bill and offers him his mother's fax machine.

## Description

Tully is about seventeen and works the hotel's front desk and lobby in the evening. When Andrew asks his name, he says it is Talila, apologizes that it is a family name, and says he goes by Tully; Andre describes a single tear *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*.

## History

On the night of the Clinton surveillance story, Andrew took a phone call from [Big Bill](big-bill.md) in the hotel lobby. Big Bill demanded an inventory of men and equipment and an itinerary by fax, and Andrew, flustered, volunteered the body count: two dead kids, the exploded man in the cave, the executed man in the Chewbacca costume and one missing man. Big Bill told him to say nothing to anyone and said he would brief the President and come down himself *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*.

Tully had overheard. He asked whether five people were dead and whether the state police being in town was connected, and Andrew denied both. When Andrew asked for a computer or fax machine, Tully refused on behalf of the hotel but offered to call his mom, who had one. Andrew declined and asked him to keep quiet about the call, which Tully agreed to do. Andrew left with finger guns, and Tully saluted them. The team instead planned to fax the itinerary from the sheriff's station *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*.
