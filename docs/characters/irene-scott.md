# Irene Scott

**Irene Scott** is the chatty, friendly mother of [Lena Scott](lena-scott.md), who runs Scott Insurance from the family home in [Marblehead](../locations/marblehead.md).

## Description

Irene is young, friendly and talkative. She offers visitors coffee, pop, snack cakes and Necco Wafers *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md))*.

## History

### The Summer Service

[Kurt Larson](kurt-larson.md) phones the number listed for "Lana Scott" from the missing scientists' contact list, reasoning that an individual is more likely a recent arrival than a long-rooted family. Irene answers "Scott Insurance, we do boats and cars." She says her daughter was on a ventilator for a couple of hours at the big hospital in Salem and is back at school, and that the scientists drew blood, promised follow-up and vanished. Kurt claims to be an FBI consultant, and Irene asks if it is terrorism *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md))*.

At the Scott home, the sign reads "Scott Insurance, Boat Specialty." Irene asks to see ID. She and her husband [Douglas Scott](douglas-scott.md), who says he hoped to hear back from the scientists, describe Lena's collapse on the beach the day after the blast *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md))*.

At the [lighthouse](../locations/lighthouse.md), Irene recognizes [Andrew Howard](andrew-howard.md) ("Mr. Howard, right?") and asks whether the team is reopening the lighthouse. After the team's warnings, the family flees *([Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md))*.
