# Willow Greengrass

**Willow Greengrass** is a member of the [Covenant](../organizations/covenant.md) in Auraria, Georgia, who vanished while carrying a sack of peaches through the woods, and whose disappearance, alongside those of two boys, brings the task force to Dahlonega.

## Description

Willow is described through others. Her sister [Primenthol Greengrass](primenthol-greengrass.md) says she was thin from malnutrition, had no money, no enemies, no suitors and no friends outside the Covenant, and had little "agency" ("she had that forehead") *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*. The cold open of the arc shows a young woman in an ankle-length dress hauling a sack of peaches through the Chattahoochee-Oconee National Forest; something hairy and inhuman scuttles between the trees and a growl drags her off-screen. The scene does not name her, but the peaches match the sack later found in the woods near Auraria *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*.

## Background

Prim says their mother joined the Covenant when the girls were small and both were renamed at about age three. Prim left, finished school and returned about a month before the disappearance to coax Willow out. Their mother has since died *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

## History

### The disappearance

The FBI dossier said an adult woman had disappeared "yesterday" when it was handed over on Monday, October 4. Andrew noticed that she vanished in [Auraria](../locations/auraria.md), which was not on their map and was treated in the file as part of Dahlonega. Sheriff [Carter Burke](sheriff-carter-burke.md) thought she had simply left the cult. The only evidence is a [bag of peaches](../items/bag-of-peaches.md) found in the middle of the woods, now in the sheriff's evidence locker *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*.

### What the Covenant said

On the trail near Auraria the team met the bootlegger [Frotter Comstock](frotter-comstock.md), who knew only that she had gone missing *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*. At the Covenant itself, the young woman Clover told Kurt that Willow had a persistent yeast infection, common in the camp, and that [Elijah Ray](elijah-ray.md) had sent her to scout peach orchards and she never returned. Willow's friend River said she was upset because her sister kept pressing her to leave, that the two had a big fight a couple of days before she vanished, and that Elijah Ray discouraged contact with family. River did not think Prim was a murderer. She said Willow had been scouting a new orchard when she disappeared *([Appalachia 04](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/04.md))*. [Monica Cartwright](monica-cartwright.md) also asked the team whether they had found "that cult lady" *([Appalachia 04](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/04.md))*.

## What remains unknown

Willow's fate is never resolved in the recorded sessions. She is not mentioned in Appalachia 09 or Walkers 01, and the arc's finale was never recorded. See [Unresolved mysteries](../lore/unresolved-mysteries.md).
