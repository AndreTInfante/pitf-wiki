# Maggie

**Maggie** is the young, blunt bartender of Ballard, Utah, who gives the team their best picture of the town's gossip, tells the ball-lightning story and mothers Andrew Howard with root beer floats.

## Description

Maggie is young, attractive and blunt, and fiercely protective of the bar's licensing. She is profane ("pardon my French"), fixes the bar's cassette jukebox, and keeps order: "Not in the bar. Not again... Parking lot." She introduces herself only at the end of the night: "I'm Maggie, by the way. Not that anyone asks." *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*

## History

After meeting [Sheriff McDermish](sheriff-mcdermish.md), the team goes to the nervously quiet bar across from the courthouse. Maggie glares at [Andrew Howard](andrew-howard.md) and demands ID, saying "I had to make sure it wasn't another Mormon fed." She makes him drink a free vodka-Coke shot, then softens and gives him a root beer float, and later half-gallon milkshakes on the house, waving off his attempt to pay. The Biz coins "Half-Gallon Howard" *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*.

She recognizes [the Biz](dick-baxter.md) from TV as the man who "spied on Bill Clinton" and gave a weird interview, and remembers [Huell](huell-kendrick.md) on the couch looking like an armed hostage. She says, "I don't think Bill Clinton would be caught dead in Ballard, Utah." She also serves the sheriff red wine in a pint glass of ice ("Coming up, you fucking freak") *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*. See [Clinton Surveillance Scandal](../lore/clinton-surveillance-scandal.md).

Maggie's gossip includes:

- The community center is being built half the county away from the community, pushed by [Deborah Wells](deborah-wells.md), whom she has known since the first grade and dislikes; Pueblo ruins were bulldozed for it despite a protest. See [Community center](../locations/community-center.md).
- Ballard has about 30 blackouts a year, four new school computers that never worked, a stink at the high school, and people who can get a college degree "magically disappear." See [Disappearances in Ballard](../lore/ballard-disappearances.md).
- The crazy rich guy renting the ranch refused a drink in a weird way and warned her off the water.
- She saw what she thinks was ball lightning streak past her car on the ranch's back road and shoot straight up; the man with her swears they nearly got abducted *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*. See [The dusk event](../lore/dusk-electrical-event.md).

When a drunk Huell tells her the town water has semen in it, the party half-confirms it, and she reasons it must be bulk-bought animal semen with a paper trail *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*. See [Ballard water contamination](../lore/ballard-water-contamination.md).
