# Doug

**Doug** is the tendril-mouthed man of two Walkers in Skin cold opens: a large, dead-eyed trailer-dweller in the Utah desert who eats Jell-O and passes grey tendrils from his mouth, in the second case into a woman's mouth.

!!! note "Name"
    The name "Doug" is spoken only in [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md). The man in the [Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md) cold open is never named, but the two scenes share the same trailer details, the Jell-O, a wrapped figure in a chair and the same tendrils, so they are treated here as one man.

## Description

In the Walkers 04 cold open, a pale, morbidly obese man in a wife beater walks through the desert with a shovel to a trailer whose lights come on automatically. The entryway holds a large cross and a mirror. He takes something from the fridge and sits in a recliner; a human-shaped figure wrapped in something shiny is propped in the other chair. He lifts a large plate of Jell-O to his chin, and fine grey tendrils spool out of his mouth onto it while his dead eyes watch the muted TV *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*.

In Walkers 06 a woman arrives at a desert trailer, takes the spare key from under a ceramic frog, and finds a pile of mail, the TV on low, cabinets of hundreds of Jell-O packages and a fridge of Jell-O plates with rotting food shoved to the back. She says, "What the fuck, Doug." She finds a human-shaped figure wrapped in black garbage bags propped in a chair, with flies audible. Then a big man in a white tank top strikes her, lifts her and kisses her. His eyes are flat black like a shark's, and grey tendrils pour from his mouth into hers while she screams *([Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*.

## History

Both appearances are cold opens without the player characters, and each ends with a cut to the credits. See [The show](../lore/the-show.md) for the cold-open format. The trailer, the Jell-O and the wrapped figure recur in both scenes. The wrapped figures resemble the [Plastic-wrapped bodies](../monsters/plastic-wrapped-bodies.md) seen elsewhere in the arc.

## What remains unknown

- How Doug's tendrils, the Jell-O and the sugar hoarding in Ballard relate. See [Sugar clues](../lore/sugar-clues.md).
- Whether he is a [Skinwalker](../monsters/skinwalkers.md). The later [Andrew Howard duplicate](andrew-howard-duplicate.md) also extends grey tendrils from its throat into its victim's mouth *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*.
- Where the trailer is, and whether Doug is connected to the Ballard events at all.
