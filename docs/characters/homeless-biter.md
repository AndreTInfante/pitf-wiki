# The "homeless" man

**The "homeless" man** is a ragged stranger in the alley beside the Ballard grocery store who bites Huell Kendrick's wrist, shouts "Pig!" and takes his dollar, in a town where locals insist there are no homeless people.

## Description

A ragged man whose voice "sounded like he smoked asbestos" *([Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*.

## History

At the [Ballard general store](../locations/ballard-general-store.md), a "homeless" man in the alley asks [Huell](huell-kendrick.md) for a dollar. When Huell holds it out, the man lunges and bites. Huell's Kick Some Ass roll is double ones, a 4 with +2, a failure. The man bites Huell's wrist, shouts "Pig!", grabs the dollar and runs. The listless clerk says "we don't got homeless in Ballard," fetches a first aid kit and bandages him *([Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*. Huell later tells the others that if they meet a homeless person they should not give him money, and Ezra jokes that the "hobo" must be the one leaving the anonymous notes *([Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*.

In [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md) Huell's hand is swelling from the bite (he says, "It'll buff"). A private handout to Rita explains the faction's method: it clones people from tissue samples, Andrew's from the cut made by the mayor's ring and Huell's from the "hobo bite" *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*. The bite is therefore the source of tissue for [Skinwalker Huell](../monsters/skinwalker-huell.md). See [Replication](../lore/replication-process.md).
