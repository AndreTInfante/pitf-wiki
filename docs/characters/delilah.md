# Delilah

**Delilah** is the red-haired, camcorder-carrying member of the [Circle Chasers](../organizations/circle-chasers.md), a teenage Bigfoot-hunting group in Dahlonega, Georgia, who films the rabbit snatch and eats heroic quantities of spuds.

## Description

Delilah is red-haired and hauls around a big, expensive VHS camcorder to document the chase. She wears flats rather than hiking boots *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

## History

### Appalachia 01

The team meets the Circle Chasers outside their trailer. Sloth introduces himself, Eddie, Vanessa, Delilah and the dog Harvey as they explain how they triangulated sightings between town and Auraria. A redheaded woman films the team the whole time with a big home video camera and tells them to "pretend like I'm not here" *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*.

### Appalachia 02

The Chasers arrive at the rabbit-snatch area after a howl in the woods. Delilah rewinds the camcorder and shows the team a three-second clip of the flashlight darting across the trail, ending with a rabbit frozen on the path and a gray man-sized blur grabbing it. Kurt notes each person's footwear to rule them out as fakers: hiking boots for the men and Vanessa, and flats for Delilah *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

### Appalachia 07

At the [original Spud Hut](../locations/original-spud-hut.md), Delilah is told she needs ID for a margarita spud. Kurt orders one and immediately hands it to her. The Biz orders two bacon burger spuds, eats about one and a quarter, and passes the rest to Delilah when she asks. She also devours four chicken pot pie spuds back to back, declaring that "if it's on the garbage can, it is a cheat day" *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*.
