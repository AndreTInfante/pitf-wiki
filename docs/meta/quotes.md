# Quotes

This page collects the best lines of *PITF*, grouped by arc in story order and then by episode. Each entry names the player, the character they are speaking as, and the moment. Lines marked "out of character" were said by the player at the table and not by a character. Every non-player character is voiced by Andre, the game master.

An ellipsis marks a place where a line has been trimmed or where someone else at the table cut in. The unrecorded sessions have no quotes. For the jokes that recur across episodes, see [Running gags](running-gags.md).
