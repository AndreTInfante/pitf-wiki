# Tropes

!!! quote
    "I have to go into work. The Protestants are trying to kill my detectives."

    Andre (as Big Bill), summing up the premise on his way out of bed *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*

***PITF*** is an early-1990s network drama about the [Paranatural Incident Task Force](../organizations/paranatural-incident-task-force.md), a tiny [FBI](../organizations/fbi.md) unit staffed by four people nobody else wanted: a true believer raised in a UFO church, a divorcing skeptic, a constipated con artist with a badge, and a Vietnam-era spook with drugs in his teeth. It is also, depending on which side of the screen you sit on, a *Monster of the Week* campaign run by [Andre](../players/andre.md) for [Rita](../players/rita.md), [Zack](../players/zack.md), [Ezra](../players/ezra.md) and [Dante](../players/dante.md), with a rotating bench of [guest players](../players/guests.md). See [The show](../lore/the-show.md) for how seriously the table takes the framing (answer: there are credits).

Across six arcs the team investigates a moth in Virginia, worms in Massachusetts, ape-men under a potato company in Georgia, body snatchers in Utah, a "Grey" in a Rhode Island attic, and then the moth again, because nothing in [Radford](../locations/radford-virginia.md) ever stays dealt with. Along the way they fire one tactical nuclear weapon, lose rental cars at an alarming rate, and live through a 1992 election that [Ross Perot](../characters/ross-perot.md) wins.

Somewhere in the middle there is also an anime. It has [its own section](#tropes-for-philosophers-fist-3j2-unchainable).

Examples below are sorted alphabetically and cited by episode. Beware of unmarked spoilers: this page gives away every twist the campaign has, including the one the players did not know about.

## A–C

- **Abandoned Mine**: [Dahlonega](../locations/dahlonega.md)'s tourist gold mine swallows a boy on a guided tour and leaves his Game Boy on the floor. Then the team falls through a boarded floor into a [hidden shaft](../locations/hidden-modern-tunnel.md) from the electric era, which is worse. *([Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md))*
- **Affably Evil**: [Jan Martense](../characters/jan-martense.md) explains his ape-man breeding scheme with the cheerful candor of a man describing a kitchen remodel. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*
- **Agent Mulder**: [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md), who keeps a mental list of UFO shapes and quizzes witnesses from it. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md))*
    - Inverted with [Walt "Coyote" Murphy](../characters/walt-murphy.md), whose explanation for a thing that fell burning out of the sky is ball lightning. Half an hour of Andrew's UFO case histories gets him as far as grudging respect. *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*
- **Agent Scully**: [Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) and [the Biz](../characters/dick-baxter.md) start as the doubters. Huell holds out until he is hanging off an astronaut that something is lifting into the sky; the Biz is officially "on board" by Georgia. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Radford Horror 05](../episodes/radford-horror/05.md), [Radford Horror 06](../episodes/radford-horror/06.md), [Appalachia 04](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/04.md))*
    - [Daniel Webster](../characters/daniel-webster.md) inherits the role in the final arc, and his notebook soon contains the line "Andrew Howard is a vampire?" *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*
- **Alien Abduction**: [Phoebe Augustine](../characters/phoebe-augustine.md) is taken, though not by tractor beam: something swoops down and lifts her. Years later [Billy Thornton](../characters/billy-thornton.md) tells an abduction story nobody will listen to. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md), [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*
- **Alien Autopsy**: The examiner opens a Ballard corpse and finds it is not human. *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*
- **Aliens Steal Cattle**: The Utah case opens with exploded sheep and mutilated cattle at the [Sherman Ranch](../locations/sherman-ranch.md). The previous FBI team blamed lightning and drink. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md), [Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*
- **The Alleged Car**: A proud lineage.
    - The task force's shabby [1961 Lincoln Continental](../items/lincoln-continental.md). *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*
    - [Betsy](../items/betsy.md), a loaner squad car that smells of "high school sadness" and comes with *Cats* costumes. *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md), [Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*
    - Aerial variant: [Freak](../characters/freak.md)'s house-painted Cessna "Annabelle", which has a disco ball, misfires, and barely clears a pine on takeoff. *([Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md))*
- **Alternate History**: Perot wins in 1992, fires the FBI director, and puts [Rudy Giuliani](../characters/rudy-giuliani.md) in the chair. See [Alternate history](../lore/alternate-history.md). *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- **Anachronism Stew**: Averted with a vengeance. Andre fact-checks HIPAA, OxyContin, Tasers and the purple Game Boy mid-session, while Kurt says "rizz" in 1992 and gets called on it. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md), [Radford Horror 05](../episodes/radford-horror/05.md), [Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md), [Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*

- **Apocalypse Cult**: The [Sons of Adam](../organizations/sons-of-adam.md), with sea baptisms, a Revelation 13 sermon and a town council waiting for the second coming. *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md), [Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md))*

- **Nightmare Fuel**: The plastic-wrapped figure that moves; corpses falling from a gym ceiling; a farmer vomiting his brain into a tank. *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*
