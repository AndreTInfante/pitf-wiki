# Game mechanics

*PITF* is played with ***Monster of the Week***, a game about hunting monsters in which the game master is called the Keeper and each player character is built from a playbook. This page covers the rules as they are used at this table: each character's playbook, ratings and moves, how Luck, harm and experience have come up, the house rulings, and the rolls that changed the story.

Everything here is what was said aloud during the sessions. Character sheets are never shown, so a rating or move appears below only if someone read it out.

## The basics

- **The roll.** Two six-sided dice plus a rating. A 10 or more is a full success, 7 to 9 is a mixed success, and 6 or less is a miss. Ezra asks how rolls work in the first session, and as late as the second arc Zack is reminding the table that the dice are "2d6, it's different." *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*
- **Experience.** A missed roll marks experience. Andre opens the second session with the reminder that "we need to remember to do the experience stuff when you guys screw up a roll." *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md))*
- **The basic moves** heard in play are Act Under Pressure, Help Out, Investigate a Mystery, Kick Some Ass, Manipulate Someone, Protect Someone, Read a Bad Situation and Use Magic.
- **Ratings** are Charm, Cool, Sharp, Tough and Weird.
- **Advanced moves.** A basic move can be marked as advanced on a level-up, which unlocks a better result on a 12 or more. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md), [Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*
- **Combat.** Andre's warning in the second session: "*Monster of the Week* combat tends to turn into like a slugging match where people die really quickly unless you're being kind of tactical about it." He recommends Help Out as the way to contribute to a fight without taking damage. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md))*

The non-canon one-shot uses a different game. See [The one-shot](#the-one-shot) below.

## Luck

"Everybody starts with seven." A point of Luck turns a roll into a success, and it can be spent after the dice are seen: asked whether that is allowed "after you roll and it's not good," Andre says, "Yeah, yeah, no, you are." A character who runs out is Doomed. *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md), [Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*

## Harm

Characters have seven harm boxes. A character over four harm is unstable and keeps getting worse until someone stabilizes them. *([Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md), [Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))*

- **Healing** has come from Andrew's Lay on Hands, Huell's first aid move and hospitals. *([Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md), [Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*
- **Lasting injuries** carry penalties. After Huell breaks his arm, Zack rolls Tough at "probably minus one because of my arm." *([Radford Horror 06](../episodes/radford-horror/06.md), [Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md))*
- **The worst night.** At the church in [Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md) Kurt reached five harm and the Biz was unstable as well. Rita: "So we just half-TPK'd." *([Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md))*
- **Small harms.** Being hit with a Taser is one harm. *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*

## Magic

Magic at this table has mostly come through Andrew's Divine moves rather than the basic Use Magic move: Lay on Hands, Cast Out Evil and his holy weapon. When Andre first asks for a Use Magic roll, to heal Kurt, Rita corrects him: "No, it's Lay On Hands. It's my actual move." *([Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md), [Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

**Use Magic** proper is rolled in [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md), by which time Andrew is the Monstrous and has dropped his Divine moves. Rita rolls a 10 and adds Weird +3 for a 13. Andre reads out the effects she can choose from: inflict harm; enchant a weapon; do one thing that is beyond human limitations; bar a place or portal; trap a specific person; banish a spirit; summon a monster into the world; communicate with something that you do not share a language with; observe another time or place; heal one harm from an injury, or cure a disease, or neutralize a poison. Zack's advice: "Summon the monster!" *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

For how the supernatural works in the fiction, see [The paranatural](../lore/the-paranatural.md).

## House rules and rulings

### Dread

Andre added a **dread** track in [Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md), when he asked Rita to "roll for dread" during one of Andrew's dreams and told her, "You take five dread." Asked what dread is, he said, "You don't know yet. Well, you're gonna find out," and then, "I'm borrowing it from some of the Lovecraft games." In [Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md) a group roll with no complete success cost everyone five dread, and the players asked where on the sheet to write it. In [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md) it was Zack who asked whether a scene was worth any ("I've got this little marker that hasn't gone up in a long time"; "I don't think my dread has gone up since we nuked a kaiju"), and Andre gave everyone one and the Biz two. Zack prompted him again in [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md): "Remember the dread mechanic if you want to use it." What dread does when it builds up has not been shown. *([Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md), [Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*

### Other rulings

- **Two questions on a 12** before the advanced move was unlocked: "Well, you still get two." *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md))*
- **A perfect 12 required.** "If you roll a perfect 12 on a Sharp check, I will let you modify this thing to go faster. If you don't, you are going to either injure yourself or break it." *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*
- **Flat luck checks.** Andre rolls a plain 2d6 "as a luck check for you guys" when the question is whether the world cooperates. *([Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md))*
- **A secret GM roll** for Kurt's bluff against [Jan Martense](../characters/jan-martense.md): "That's not how we're doing this one. 'Cause it's more fun that way." *([Appalachia 08](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/08.md))*
- **Rolling for NPC gear.** "I am gonna do something I haven't done before here, and I am going to roll for Freak and his maintenance of the Cessna." *([Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md))*
- **Cocked dice are rerolled.** *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*
- **Proxy rolling.** When a player's dice roller is broken, Andre rolls for them. *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*
- **Rolling for casting.** Whether Chevy Chase guest-stars in the show was put to the dice. It was a mixed success. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*

## The one-shot

- **Conditions:** Afraid, Angry, Guilty, Insecure, Hopeless.
