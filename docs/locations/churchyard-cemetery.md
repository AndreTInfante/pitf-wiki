# Churchyard cemetery

**The churchyard cemetery** is the fenced graveyard in [Ballard, Utah](ballard-utah.md) where [Shelly Wilmarth](../characters/shelly-wilmarth.md) lies under an expensive black granite slab, the target of the task force's exhumation plan in *The Walkers in Skin*.

## Description

The churchyard is fenced and locked with a cheap '90s Master Lock. The gate opens with "the loudest squeal you have ever heard", and the grass is well maintained *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*.

## Notable places within

**Shelly Wilmarth's grave.** A big black granite slab lies over the grave. It is undisturbed and clean, with trimmed grass around it and some trampled grass suggesting a recent visitor *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*.

## History of events there

**The first search.** After about two hours in [the bar](ballard-bar.md), the team goes to the churchyard. [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) picks the lock, and after about half an hour of searching they find the grave. Kurt notes that they could steal the body if the exhumation is denied *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*.

**The court order.** The sheriff files the paperwork, but the judge, who objects for religious reasons, will not budge for Andrew. The task force's boss [Big Bill](../characters/big-bill.md) talks to the judge for about twenty minutes, and the exhumation is set for the day after tomorrow *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*. As a backup plan, the team rents ground-penetrating radar to check the casket first *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md), [Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*. See [Exhumation of Shelly Wilmarth](../lore/exhumation-of-shelly-wilmarth.md).

**The radar test.** In [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md) the team takes the [radar](../items/ground-penetrating-radar.md) to the cemetery. At first it will not turn on, and its wires look chewed. Zack (as [Huell](../characters/huell-kendrick.md)) repairs it with aluminum-foil gum wrappers on a roll of double sixes. Pushed over the grave and compared against neighbouring graves, it shows something solid in the coffin, and the team is about 85 percent confident of it.

**The exhumation order.** At the start of [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), Andre notes that the team has the exhumation order for the next morning. The grave is never opened in any recorded episode.

## Trivia

- In Walkers 01 Alberta says her mother died two years earlier and shows an obituary reading winter 1990, while in Walkers 05 Andre says Shelly died "about, I think, like two months ago". The episodes never reconcile the two.
