# Community center

**The community center** is the fenced cinder-block construction project, pushed by [Deborah Wells](../characters/deborah-wells.md) and Mayor [Wilford Hiram](../characters/wilford-hiram.md) on bulldozed Pueblo ruins within sight of [Sherman Ranch](sherman-ranch.md), which proves to be the [skinwalkers'](../monsters/skinwalkers.md) stronghold above the "Ballroom" shaft and collapses into a sinkhole in the finale.

## Description

From the ranch, [Walt Murphy](../characters/walt-murphy.md) points out the building going up beside a gray [substation](substation.md) built just for it, about four miles from the only part of the county where anything strange happens. The town says it will swallow the ranch area, but [Alberta](../characters/alberta-wilmarth.md), Walt says, will never sell *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*.

Up close it is most of a cinder-block building, ringed by a heavy chain-link fence topped with barbed wire, with bulldozers and a backhoe parked outside and work lights on. Mean graffiti over the construction logos on the fence accuses the builders of "cultural genocide". It stands at the end of a dirt road in empty desert, and the guest house is visible from it *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*.

## Notable places within

- **The hollow hallway.** An unguarded back door opens onto an ordinary hallway with dirt on the floor and a hollow-sounding floor *([Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*.
- **The Ballroom.** A locked door in the hallway opens onto a shaft a few hundred feet deep that leads to the [cavern](cavern.md).
- **The fenced yard.** Behind a fence that has been knocked down in several places, most of Roosevelt's cars are piled up *([Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*.

## History of events there

**Suspicion.** [Maggie](../characters/maggie.md) says the center is being built far from the community, pushed by Deborah Wells, over Pueblo ruins that were bulldozed despite a protest *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*. Rita (as Andrew) photographs the site, and the team decides not to break in yet. [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) concludes the ranch is the likely reason for the location. After meeting the mayor, Andrew and Kurt wonder whether it is a "community center scam", or a church community center moved to mine a gas reserve *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*.

**The assault.** In [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md) a convoy of Guardsmen, the task force, ranch hands on horseback and Ned Simpson's [killdozer](../items/killdozer.md) approaches from behind a hill. The APC with the megaphone is answered with gunfire. The naked defenders are tactically poor but show no fear of death, and they swarm the killdozer; Kurt sears the last two climbers off with the [flame light](../items/flame-light.md). Hoarded supplies and cars are stored here.

**The collapse.** After the team destroys the portal machine in the cavern below, the community center slides into a sinkhole while the survivors above lie unconscious from the falling white powder. Dozens of people in hazmat suits then load up bodies as black aircraft circle overhead *([Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*. See [Hazmat cleanup teams](../organizations/hazmat-cleanup-teams.md).

## Inhabitants

The skinwalker faction (copies of the people of Roosevelt and Ballard, including the mayor), who haul supplies into the center and defend it with guns *([Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*.
