# Public Library

!!! note "Non-canon"
    The Public Library belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

The **Public Library** is the library in [the Capital](pf-capital.md) whose downstairs children's room is where [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) draws his flag.

## Description

Mirabeth picks the library from the video phone's satellite map as a place where Derk can draw while she travels. The children's room is downstairs. Butcher paper is unrolled over the floor, and a table holds ham-and-cheese sandwiches, a few PB&Js and a pile of juice boxes. A large wishing-well fountain with coins in the bottom stands outside. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History of events there

Derk lands outside the library after his jump from the [Athletes' Village](pf-athletes-village.md), cracking the paving stones, and washes in the fountain. A librarian asks him to use his "inside voice," so he answers with his hand over his mouth, and the staff sit him down in the children's room next to a group of toddlers. He eats just under half the snack table and, according to the staff, apparently mistakes a snow globe for a juice box. Over the next hour and a half he draws scorpions, the stars seen from space and a dog on the floor paper, a Superior roll of 6. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

The staff, who found him unsupervised and unable to explain himself, call child services and [Animal Control](../organizations/pf-animal-control.md). When [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) arrives about an hour later, the front desk is empty, the toddlers are crying in a corner and every member of the staff is staring at Derk. She sends Derk off along the emergency exit signs at speed and tears the cape-sized paper off the floor, which becomes [Derk's Flag](../items/pf-derks-flag.md). She walks past Animal Control and out the front door while police sirens approach. Later a detective asks her about the incident and says the Animal Control call ended in a riot, adding that the agency was sick of prank calls. What happened in the riot is never explained. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
