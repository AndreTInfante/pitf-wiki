# Dahlonega, Georgia

**Dahlonega, Georgia** is a gold-rush tourist town of a few thousand people, officially the "wine tasting room capital of Georgia" according to the state senate, home of the [Spud Hut](../organizations/spud-hut.md) and the base for the party throughout [The Things in Appalachia](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/index.md).

## Description

A former gold-rush center, Dahlonega is a small mountain town of roughly six by seven streets, with a gold mining museum, a McDonald's, a food mart, Stigley's Pub and an Express & Suites motel; there is no Walmart. The town's tourist industry is built on its mines. The Smith Hotel desk clerk delivers a tour-guide spiel about the wine tasting and the gold mining museum *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*. Underground Gold Mine Tours advertises itself as one mile from downtown, and the town has a newsstand and a second hotel, the Hotel and Suites, which used to be a Best Western *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

## History of events there

**October 1992.** The team arrives to investigate a missing woman, [Willow Greengrass](../characters/willow-greengrass.md), and reported Bigfoot sightings, and meets the Circle Chasers, Sheriff Burke and the local color *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*. Over the arc the disappearances mount, with Willow, [Edward Nick](../characters/edward-nick.md), [Gene Cartwright](../characters/gene-cartwright.md), [Aaron Richters](../characters/aaron-richters.md) and [Frederick](../characters/frederick.md) all vanishing. The sheriff laments "too many people going missing" *([Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md))*.

**The Clinton scandal.** Presidential candidate Bill Clinton was campaigning in Dahlonega, and a Georgia State Police press conference alleged that the FBI was in town surveilling him *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*. Reporters gathered in town, and a Fox 5 crew recognized Andrew Howard live on television *([Appalachia 08](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/08.md))*. See [Clinton Surveillance Scandal](../lore/clinton-surveillance-scandal.md) and [Fox 5 Atlanta](../organizations/fox-5-atlanta.md).

**The finale.** In Appalachia 09 the party crossed the mines beneath the town, ending at a ladder exit under a tarp in the woods outside the Spud Hut plant *([Appalachia 09](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/09.md))*. The final session was never recorded; the epilogue in [Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md) says the Spud Hut facility exploded shortly after Jan fled.

## Inhabitants

Sheriff Burke, the Circle Chasers, the Covenant of nearby Auraria, [Jan Martense](../characters/jan-martense.md) and the Spud Hut staff, and the Smith Hotel clerk. The Spud Hut's perfect-recall counter worker appears as the [Spud Hut Counter Savant](../characters/spud-hut-savant.md).

## Trivia

- The Smith Hotel clerk gives the population as about 4,000 in Appalachia 01; in Appalachia 03 Andre says the real-life figure at the time was about 4,500.
