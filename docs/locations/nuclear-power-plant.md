# The nuclear power plant

**The nuclear power plant** is a fortified coastal reactor facility near [Marblehead, Massachusetts](marblehead.md), and the target of the infected convoy in the climax of The Summer Service.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Location |
| **Region** | Marblehead area, Massachusetts, on the coast |
| **Status** | Fence knocked down and machine guns silenced by the nuclear blast; infected people were inside and fighting continued (as of Summer Service 06) |
| **First appearance** | [Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md) |

## Description

The plant lies beyond a flooded freeway and a stretch of marshland, ringed by a perimeter fence. When Kurt worries about firing a nuclear device near it, the [radio contact](../characters/radio-contact.md) replies, "Oh, those things are way overbuilt. It'll be fine. They're designed for you to fly a 747 into 'em." Kurt doubts his claim to have "done this before" *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.

The episodes never name the plant or the real-world facility it might be modeled on.

## Defenses

Just beyond the fence the ground drops about six inches when something heavy crosses it, which popped dozens of tires on the convoy's vehicles. Machine-gun turrets then cut the convoy down from behind the rain and fog. A handful of howling infected ran through the gunfire and got inside, and one gun jammed with a horrible mechanical sound *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.

## History of events there

- **The plan.** After the glowing [angel thing](../monsters/angel-thing.md) turned back toward the sea, the radio contact and Kurt agreed the creature was heading for the plant, where the convoy of [worm-infected](../monsters/worm-bags.md) townspeople was going. The idea was to let the convoy engage plant security, which would force the creature out of the water to help them, and then to take a shot at it from a vantage point *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.
- **The convoy attack.** The team followed the convoy along the freeway, then watched it leave the road for the last quarter mile through marsh. From about a mile away they saw the convoy ram the fence, lose its tires to the sunken ground and get shredded by the machine guns *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.
- **The nuclear shot.** As a light rose out of the ocean, Huell fired the [Davy Crockett](../items/davy-crockett.md). The shockwave knocked the team flat, toppled the fence and silenced the guns, and trees in the distance burst into flame. The creature survived and came straight for the team *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.
- **Aftermath.** Gunfire could still be heard from the plant as the team left. At the Marblehead checkpoint a National Guard lieutenant told Andrew that the attack had failed but killed a few guards. The older officer in charge later remarked that someone had used a tactical nuke there, which would be very hard to keep out of the news. The team agreed to tell the authorities that terrorists were responsible ([Terrorist cover story](../lore/terrorist-cover-story.md)) *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md))*.

## Inhabitants

Plant security and the infected who breached it. It is never shown how many infected survived inside.
