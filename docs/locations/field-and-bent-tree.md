# The Field and Bent Tree

**The Field and Bent Tree** is the overgrown field behind the Dahlonega trailer park where Gene Cartwright, a boy of about seven, was taken, and where the team found his shoe hung high in a tree.

## Description

The field lies behind the double-wide park where the Nick family lives, and a couple dozen kids play there. When the team arrived it was knee-high grass, thick with mosquitoes and biting flies, ringed with police tape and trampled by the volunteers of the manhunt *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

Edward Nick's mother directed the team to the spot by landmark. From her window, a big rock that looks like a man's head, which she calls "Injun Rock," has a nose that points toward a bent tree at the wood line. Edward had been right by that tree when the family heard the screaming *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

## Notable places within

- **Injun Rock.** The head-shaped rock; following its nose leads to the bent tree.
- **The bent tree.** Gene's red, white-soled shoe was hung high in its branches, with a tuft of pale, soft, body-like hair (ends intact) caught in the laces.

## History of events there

Kurt proposed an inverted-wedge search formation, with the people in the back rechecking the footsteps of those in front. Because the volunteers had trampled the ground, Investigate a Mystery was at -1; the Biz still rolled well enough, with a boost from Kurt, to ask two questions. His first, "What sort of creature is it?", revealed something dangling from a high branch. Huell climbed the sketchy limb (Act Under Pressure, a mixed result), passed the roll to avoid falling as the branch cracked, and ruined his suit retrieving the shoe. Kurt and Huell concluded the boy was dead *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

The Biz's second question, "Where did it go?", was answered by Edward's account, relayed by his mother: something appeared from nowhere, grabbed him and ran into the woods. Andrew pointed out that this did not explain a shoe in a tree. The shoe and hair were bagged for McManley. Kurt phoned Sheriff Burke, who confirmed that the missing boy's shoe was red with a white sole, and who added that he needed to talk to his deputies about looking up when surveying a scene *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*. See [Gene's Shoe](../items/genes-shoe.md).

A different child's shoe, German in style, was later found in the [hidden modern tunnel](hidden-modern-tunnel.md); see [Child's Tennis Shoe](../items/childs-tennis-shoe.md).
