# The island across the bay

**The island across the bay** is the wealthy part of [Marblehead](marblehead.md), home to Castle Rock and a bird sanctuary, reached by a bridge that floods during the storm; [Arnold Weathers](../characters/arnold-weathers.md) lives there.

## Description

In Summer Service 01, Kurt asks a local about the island he can see across the water. The local replies that it is not really an island, since a highway connects it to town, and Dante jokes that it is a "man-made peninsula". It is the nice part of town, with Castle Rock (worth seeing for tourists), lobster fishing boats and a bird sanctuary *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md))*.

By Summer Service 05 the connection is a bridge that the storm has all but washed out: wind whips the ocean across the pavement, and cars are up to mid-wheel in seawater for much of the crossing. The homes beyond are rich, with high walls and privacy *([Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*.

## Notable places within

- [Arnold Weathers' house](arnold-weathers-house.md), an eight-foot-walled estate with a padlocked gate.
- Castle Rock and the bird sanctuary, named but not visited.

## History of events there

### Summer Service 05

Summer Service 05 is the only visit. During the storm, [Madeline Weathers](../characters/madeline-weathers.md) leads the team in a Volkswagen Beetle across the flooded bridge. Huell drives the team's car, shakes a tail by driving through the storm, narrowly misses an elderly couple's station wagon and hydroplanes across a one-way street. On the island they find Arnold's house padlocked and silent, and later destroy him and the infected dog Puddles there *([Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*.

## Inhabitants

Wealthy residents and Arnold Weathers.
