# Brooks Pharmacy

**The Brooks Pharmacy** is a one-story strip-mall pharmacy in a town about 25 miles from Exeter, Rhode Island, where a masked gunman takes hostages in the finale of The Roulet Estate.

## Description

The store has wraparound laminated-glass windows, one of them riddled with bullet holes, and a door on the corner set at about 45 degrees; Rita compares it to a CVS. A service entrance at the back is kept locked by someone named Markeith. An access ladder for roof maintenance, currently retracted and six or seven feet up, leads to a roof door and a cramped crawl space full of air-conditioning equipment above a drop ceiling. Inside, the pharmacy area has been trashed, with plastic tubs and pill bottles scattered across the floor, and a checkout counter stands near the front. A parking lot lies in front, with snow on the ground. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

## History of events there

- **The arrival.** A local cop reports that the agents' stolen car has been spotted at the Brooks. When the agents arrive, it is parked beside a squad car with two cops hiding behind it. The [young balding cop](../characters/young-balding-cop.md) is shot dead as he steps out of the car. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
- **The briefing.** [Officer Todd](../characters/officer-todd.md) surrenders command to Andrew and relays the gunman's demands: a fueled Range Rover with no trackers, no police cars in the lot, and all cops inside within ten minutes, naked, to be handcuffed to shopping carts. A businessman hostage runs out carrying a brick-sized cellular phone and recites a number, 401-998-3147, for the agents to call. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
- **The plan.** Shots from the store flatten the tires of the car the agents are pushing, so Kurt strips a popped tire, soaks it in a flask's worth of vodka and stuffs in a bag of crusty donuts from the squad car to make a smoke screen. Andrew stalls the gunman on the phone, asking whether a Ford Bronco will work, and takes off his shirt after being told to strip, which draws the [old woman in the VW Beetle](../characters/old-woman-in-the-vw-beetle.md). The Biz climbs the ladder, picks the roof door lock with a credit card and creeps through the drop ceiling. Kurt picks the back door and creeps toward the counter, where a hostage's gaze tells him where the gunman is. Andre estimates 20 or 30 hostages from the shapes in the window and the crowd outside. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
- **The fight.** The Biz shoots first through the ceiling and is hit in the thigh by a Mosin-Nagant round, falling to the tile. Huell tackles the gunman and takes a shot through the foot. Kurt binds him. Police remove the [alien mask](../items/alien-mask.md), and the man, revealed as the [Roulet Intruder](../monsters/roulet-intruder.md), is given beta blockers when he begins to collapse. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

## Inhabitants

The pharmacist (shot non-fatally), a businessman, a customer with a gut wound, and the other hostages. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
