# The Weapons Closet

**The Weapons Closet** is a panic-room-style gun room in the [Roulet House](roulet-house.md), sealed behind a steel door and emptied of nearly all its firearms before the agents arrive.

## Description

The door sits in the living room, near the television and the VHS rack, and has a combination lock like the door to a panic room. Nobody has the combination. Through a gap beneath it Andrew sees empty racks on the walls, which Andre says once held about twenty firearms, and a single World War II flamethrower left against the rear wall *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*.

## History of events there

Kurt bashes at the wooden floor beside the door with the iron stand that holds the fireplace tools, for about forty-five minutes, while the others watch the windows. During the hammering Rita (as Andrew) steps outside and shouts "Can we just talk?" into the woods, then glimpses a low, brilliant light streaking through the trees; when she turns to look, it is gone. When the noise stops, the others find Kurt staring at the door, dizzy, having lost a minute of time. Andrew takes over, the floorboards cave, and he squeezes his head and a flashlight under the door. He concludes that the couple's "arsenal closet" was "looted by our extraterrestrial neighbors" *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*.

In Roulet Estate 02 the agents find the missing guns, including a Kalashnikov and WWII-era Russian pistols, stored in the [hidden attic lair](hidden-attic-lair.md), and bring them down the chimney. Andre says the gentleman of the house seems to have been a firearms collector. The players take this to explain the sniper's [VSS](../items/vss-rifle.md) and to make a KGB plot less likely *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*. In Roulet Estate 03, [Officer Todd](../characters/officer-todd.md) reports that the pharmacy gunman has a flamethrower, a machine gun and a heavy wooden rifle *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*. It is never established whether that flamethrower is the one left in the closet.
