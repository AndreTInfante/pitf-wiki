# Minor locations: Radford (The Radford Horror and The Radford Horrors)

This page lists the minor places visited or mentioned in the two Radford arcs, from fast-food stops to a flooded colonial settlement.

### Arby's

The Radford Arby's is the team's regroup spot and the starting point of Radford Horror 03, where Andre remarks that it "might be the worst chain in America". *([Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md))* See [Running gags](../meta/running-gags.md).

### Ballard, Utah ranch

The ranch in Ballard, Utah is mentioned in Moscow, where the Biz explains skinwalkers to Yeltsin: they mimic well but are not strategic, they turn erratic and violent when confronted, and the older ones are more convincing than new ones. It is the site of the Utah case, covered in [Sherman Ranch](sherman-ranch.md) and [Ballard, Utah](ballard-utah.md). *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*

### Blockbuster Video

The back employee room of a Blockbuster Video, with a tiny VHS TV, is where the team views the diner's raid tape and has it copied by Harold. *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))* See [Harold](../characters/harold.md) and [Diner security tape](../items/diner-security-tape.md).

### Dirt road and clearing

The dirt road midway down the mountain leads to a clearing where an armed group is camped: five or six Spanish-speaking people with flashlights, at least one shotgun and an idling vehicle. Dean Lawson's Miata came from the same area. *([Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md))* See [Armed group in the woods](../organizations/armed-group-in-the-woods.md) and [Mountain road hit-and-run site](mountain-road-site.md).

### Dog's backyard

A suburban backyard, a few blocks from the Augustines, holds a doghouse with a snapped leash where a dog was taken at about 3 a.m. It is where the team finds the white powder. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md))* See [White powder](../items/white-powder.md) and [Radford's missing dogs](../lore/missing-dogs.md).

### FBI headquarters

The FBI building in Washington is where the team is reassigned, with a smoky canteen, crowded elevators and winding corridors. In 1993 it is tense after Sessions's firing: the team waits outside the Director's office, Todd talks to Kurtis in the men's room, and Giuliani briefs the team in the Director's office. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))* See [FBI](../organizations/fbi.md) and [Rudy Giuliani](../characters/rudy-giuliani.md).

### Hoboken motel

A motel in Jersey City, in the vicinity of Hoboken, is the non-horrifying but expensive overnight stop on the drive to Marblehead. *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))* See [Marblehead](marblehead.md).

### Huell's apartment

Huell's flat is squalid: Roseanne is on, takeout covers the area around the TV, phone books sit on chairs, the microwave is full of dishes and there is a melted Pop-Tart in the toaster. Huell convalesces here after his leg injury. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))* See [Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md).

### McDonald's drive-thru

The Radford McDonald's has a drive-thru wrapping three sides of the building, with the town's only Radio Shack across the street. The team commandeers the elderly couple's car here, and the drive-thru voice offers to dino-size the order for $11.95. It is also a stakeout food stop and the scene of the earlier carjacking. *([Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md), [Radford Horror 06](../episodes/radford-horror/06.md))* See [Howard and his wife](../characters/elderly-couple.md).

### Post office

The roof of the Radford post office is used by an unknown watcher, and its parking lot holds the plates Kurt memorizes. The Marblehead post office also appears in The Summer Service, where McManley's mobile lab is parked. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md), [Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md))* See [Rooftop watcher](../characters/rooftop-watcher.md).

### Quest Coffee

Quest Coffee is the upstart coffee shop in Radford. When Kurt jokes about artisanal coffee shops, the sheriff names the only two coffee places in town: the diner and Quest Coffee. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md))*

### Radford church

The Radford church hosts the town's community meeting at 4:30 p.m., with the sheriff, the FBI and the priest speaking in that order; the town hall is out because of a roof leak and black mold. Molly briefs the team in the back break room beforehand. It is not the old-town church of the climax. *([Radford Horror 05](../episodes/radford-horror/05.md))* See [Old-town church](old-town-church.md).

### Radford family-medicine clinic

The family-medicine clinic is the doctor's office in town, where the team takes the heroin sample for testing. *([Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md))* See [Dean Lawson's heroin and cash](../items/deans-heroin-and-cash.md).

### Radford old town and mill

The colonial old town is a flooded, abandoned settlement with stone foundations around the church. A mill a quarter mile to the west survives, overgrown, and the team does not find the heroin there. *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))* See [Radford old town flood](../lore/minor-radford.md#radford-old-town-flood) and [Old-town church](old-town-church.md).

### Radford Sheriff's station

The small station has a broom-closet records archive of shelves, boxes and filing cabinets, where Andrew finds the Radio Shack file and the missing-dog folder. Its small morgue, with nitrile gloves, linen aprons and a Vicks tray, is used for Amanda's autopsy. In 1993 the station is lit up at night, and Molly keeps files and the posters there. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md), [Radford Horror 05](../episodes/radford-horror/05.md), [Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))* See [Radford Sheriff's Department](../organizations/radford-sheriffs-department.md).

### Radio Shack

The Radford Radio Shack was the site of Abe's spacesuit break-in three years before the story. The robbery file describes a cinder-block break-in, the spacesuit, fingerprints from heavy gear, stolen modems and a cell phone, and a bus ticket to Richmond. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md), [Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md))* See [Radio Shack spacesuit robbery](../lore/radio-shack-robbery.md).

### River near the church

The river near the old-town church is where the team tests the heat-ray flashlight. *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))* See [Flashlight weapon](../items/flashlight-weapon.md).

### Roanoke Airport

Roanoke Airport is the nearest airport, about 45 minutes from Radford by road. It is a small regional airport with 737s and private traffic, ringed by mountains; Freak's Cessna is parked in a corner. *([Radford Horror 02](../episodes/radford-horror/02.md), [Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md), [Radford Horror 07](../episodes/radford-horror/07.md))* See [Freak](../characters/freak.md) and [Annabelle](../items/minor-radford.md#annabelle).

### Roanoke hospital

The hospital in Roanoke runs the DFA test on the rabid rat that bit Webster and supplies the HDCV vaccine. *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))* See [Rabid mouse](../monsters/rabid-mouse.md).

### Salem, Virginia

Salem is the town of the ultralight owner, off to the right of the team's search area. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md))*

### Science fair gymnasium

The school gym, with foam boards, potato batteries and a volcano, is where Kurtis meets Toby Garland. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))* See [Peggy Browning](../characters/minor-radford.md#peggy-browning).

### State police headquarters

State police headquarters holds Derek Augustine after the rescue of Dr. Lori. *([Radford Horror 06](../episodes/radford-horror/06.md))* See [Derek Augustine](../characters/derek-augustine.md).

### Tyler's trailer

Tyler's trailer sits about 100 feet from the Lucky house, across a wide road and deep yard. The living room is where Tyler is interviewed. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))* See [Tyler](../characters/tyler.md) and [Lucky house](lucky-house.md).

### Underhill's mother-in-law suite

The rear suite of the sheriff's home is offered to the team, and accepted, as lodging after the Pay n' Stay. *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))* See [Molly Underhill](../characters/molly-underhill.md) and [Pay n' Stay](pay-n-stay.md).
