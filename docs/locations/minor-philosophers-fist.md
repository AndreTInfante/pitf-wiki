# Minor locations: Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable (non-canon one-shot)

!!! note "Non-canon"
    These places appear only in *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, a non-canon one-shot presented as an in-universe anime OVA. Nothing here is part of the main campaign's continuity.

Minor locations of the one-shot that do not have their own pages, in alphabetical order. The major sites are the [Athletes' Village](pf-athletes-village.md), [The Capital (DC)](pf-capital.md), the [Presidential Event Hall](pf-presidential-event-hall.md), the [Public Library](pf-public-library.md) and [Wisukonshin](pf-wisukonshin.md).

### Community Center

The venue of the pre-event social for disadvantaged children and their guardians, ten blocks from the [Public Library](pf-public-library.md). Here [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) is insulted by a bully and befriended by Sarah, and [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) sells Addison an invented essential-oil business. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### I-95

The highway whose shoulder [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) speed-walks along in pumps to reach [the capital](pf-capital.md), about 400 miles from the athletes' village. She keeps up a dignified power-walk behind her finned parasol and goes up and over any car in her way. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### Miguel's Hot Dog Taco Stand

A street stand about a quarter mile outside the athletes' compound, where [Miguel](../characters/pf-miguel.md) sells hot dogs in taco shells with spicy cheese, tomatoes and pico de gallo. [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) and Mirabeth land beside it after leaping the compound wall, and Derk eats 98 hot dog tacos. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### Mirabeth's Home

Mirabeth's home, equipped with an in-home video phone. After her debriefing she speeds home, and Derk mostly knows where it is. It is where she takes Derk's 2 a.m. call asking to be picked up, and she sets out from here, after removing her curlers and doing her makeup, to rescue him. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### Motel 6

The leads' $86 joint suite in [the capital](pf-capital.md), one of the relatively clean ones, where they stay after a spot opens up for Derk. The television news there shows Ovain fighting the military at the capital's edge the night before the presidential event. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### West City

A financial district where four blocks are evacuated after a gas-line rupture. After the Ovain fight, Colonel Sparago lists the rupture among the damage he attributes to Derk. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
