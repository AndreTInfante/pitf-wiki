# Ram and Arms Inn

**The Ram and Arms Inn** is a decrepit old hotel, in practice a bed-and-breakfast, on Gregory Street in [Marblehead, Massachusetts](marblehead.md), where the task force lodges throughout The Summer Service.

## Description

The inn is a plain but clean old hotel with a view of the old military fort, a ram's-head coat of arms over the door and a narrow wooden staircase. The rooms are small, little more than a twin bed and a chair, with a nice window view *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))*. They have no phones or televisions, only a phone at the end of the hall. The inn stands on Gregory Street, close to the beach, and guests are served a fish breakfast at 6 a.m. *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md))*. The building has two stories, good water pressure, a hallway bathroom whose window opens onto an alley, and a small washing machine downstairs *([Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md), [Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*. Every guest is offered a Bible, including the [Sons of Adam Bible](../items/sons-of-adam-bible.md) *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))*.

## History of events there

- **Check-in.** At the end of Radford Horror 08 the team arrives and an ancient innkeeper offers four single rooms and the 6 a.m. hot fish breakfast. He says business has been quiet "except for those fellas from the government" about a week earlier, and he is keeping their belongings in a box in the back. This is the [EPA/USGS survey team](../organizations/epa-usgs-survey-team.md). The innkeeper carries the box up to the team's rooms *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md))*. See [Ram and Arms innkeeper](../characters/ram-and-arms-innkeeper.md).
- **Summer Service 01.** The team goes through the [previous team's belongings](../items/survey-teams-field-notes.md) and Andrew speed-reads the Bible. Kurt makes calls from the hallway phone once a teenage girl has finished with it. At the desk the Biz finds Jester's bag of [crystal marbles](../items/crystal-marbles.md). When Huell asks for a toothbrush, Jester tells him the inn is "a bed-and-breakfast. Not a bed-and-toothbrush." The day ends with Jester frantically rooting around his desk *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md))*.
- **Summer Service 03.** After the dive, Kurt sleeps badly in feverish chills. Jester looks haunted and gives Huell an appraising nod. When McManley says he hears the hotel serves a fish breakfast, Andrew warns, "Don't eat the fish here. The water is tainted" *([Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md))*.
- **Summer Service 04.** Jester, who is also a selectman, tells the team, "You better not be planning to leave whatever that is in the room." The roughly 60 lb W54 warhead of the [Davy Crockett](../items/davy-crockett.md) is wrapped in Kurt's poncho and stored in his room *([Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md))*.
- **Summer Service 05, the siege.** During the [tropical storm](../lore/tropical-storm-andrew-2.md), McManley takes a room because his [RV](mcmanleys-rv.md) has run out of hot water. Jester is not at the desk; McManley says he took a call in the back and practically ran out the door. In the middle of the night Andrew feels a voice at his ear telling him to get his gun. Church members gather outside in the rain and a police car pulls up. Andrew kicks in a guest's door to scout the back, then drops a toilet tank lid onto a watcher below the bathroom window and lands on him. The team passes the Davy Crockett down through that window and sneaks past the remaining watchers to the RV *([Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*.

## Inhabitants

[Jester](../characters/jester.md) works the front desk and is a member of the [Sons of Adam](../organizations/sons-of-adam.md).

## Trivia

- Radford Horror 08 is the only episode in which the inn is named. In The Summer Service it is "the hotel", "the motel" or a bed-and-breakfast.
- The innkeeper of Radford Horror 08 is a very old man with a "Crypt Keeper" haircut, and Summer Service 01 introduces Jester as the man with the Crypt Keeper haircut who "now" wears a name tag. The episodes strongly imply that they are the same man, but never state it outright.
