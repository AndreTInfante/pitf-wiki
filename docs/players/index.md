# Cast and players

*PITF* is played by one game master and four regular players, with a handful of guests who sit in on the call. This page lists who plays whom and which sessions each person is heard in.

## The regular cast

| Player | Role | Characters | Recorded sessions |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Andre](andre.md) | Game master (Keeper) | Every NPC, monster and narrator voice | All 37 canon sessions and the one-shot |
| [Rita](rita.md) | Player | [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md); briefly [his duplicate](../characters/andrew-howard-duplicate.md); Mirabeth in the one-shot | All 37 canon sessions and the one-shot |
| [Dante](dante.md) | Player | [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md); Derk in the one-shot | All 37 canon sessions and the one-shot |
| [Ezra](ezra.md) | Player | [Dick "The Biz" Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md); [Daniel Webster](../characters/daniel-webster.md) | All 37 canon sessions |
| [Zack](zack.md) | Player | [Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) | All 37 canon sessions |

All four regulars are heard in every recorded canon session, from [Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md) to [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md). The only canon session in which a regular's main character does not appear is [Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md), where the Biz is a prisoner in Russia and Ezra plays Daniel Webster instead. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md), [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

The non-canon one-shot [*Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md) has a smaller cast: Andre running, Rita and Dante playing, and a guest commenting from the sidelines. Ezra and Zack are not in it. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Uncertain speakers

Five recordings come from a single mixed audio track, so it is not always certain which player is speaking: [Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md), [Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md), [Appalachia 04](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/04.md) and [Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md). A line or a roll credited to a particular player in those sessions is probable rather than certain. See [Lost episodes](../meta/lost-episodes.md).
