# Servitors

**Servitors** are beings named by the crash survivor in Russia as the people who "need resources," the "opposite of aliens"; a fragment of a burned document in Radford uses the same word.

| **Status** | Unknown |

## Description

Nothing is known directly about servitors. The only description comes from what the [crash survivor](crash-survivor.md) says under questioning by President Yeltsin: when asked why his people are there, "The aliens," he answers "Not aliens. The opposite of aliens," and "Servitors... need resources" before passing out. *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

## Abilities

None are stated. Observations about the survivor himself: he shares fluids with a Russian soldier and accepts O-negative blood, which Yeltsin finds odd for an alien. *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

## History

In Radford Horror 04, the burned remains of [Murphy's briefcase and documents](../items/murphys-briefcase-and-documents.md) yield legible fragments, among them "Menelaus," "Big Bill," "samples," "acceptable leakage" and "unknown servitor act[ivity]." Ezra (as [Dick Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md)) mutters, "Whatever the fuck a servitor is," and later warns the team that the papers might be a trap. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md))* See [Agent Murphy](../characters/agent-murphy.md) and [Radford Moth](radford-moth.md).

In Russia, the Biz tells Yeltsin that these are not the aliens he has dealt with: he has seen resources go through portals, but it "presented differently," and he has never heard of servitors. *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

## What remains unknown

What servitors are, what "resources" they need, whether they relate to the moth, to [Andrew Howard's](../characters/andrew-howard.md) condition, or to the document fragment's "unknown servitor activity," and whether the Radford Horror 04 papers are genuine at all. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md), [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*
