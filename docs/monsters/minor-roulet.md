# Minor monsters and entities: The Roulet Estate

Minor entities of The Roulet Estate. The main creature of the arc is [The Roulet Intruder](roulet-intruder.md).

### Utah UFOs

The craft that [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md) photographed over Ballard during the events of [The Walkers in Skin](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/index.md); see also [Black triangular craft](black-triangular-craft.md). In Roulet Estate 01 Andrew carries three treasured Polaroids of them in his satchel, and when the agents discuss the UFO sightings associated with the Rhode Island house, Kurt asks whether they match what the team saw in Utah. In Roulet Estate 02 Andre recalls that the black triangles in the house's materials came from a sketch. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

### Veiny Hands and the Prison Guard

Kurt's hallucination in the basement of the [Roulet house](../locations/roulet-house.md), brought on by the [drugged water](../lore/drugged-water.md). While digging in the trash pit, [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md) sees veiny hands burst from the dirt and grab his wrists, and a Vietnamese prison guard's face appears. He fires into the hole and the guard's face crumples in on itself as other hands crawl out to grab him. He comes to curled in the fetal position, with Andrew shaking him, and says he was "back in Saigon." The hand in the hole turns out to be real: it belongs to a corpse, and a ring on it matches the wedding ring in the wedding photos, identifying [Margaret Roulet](../characters/margaret-roulet.md). Kurt's explanation is "In my defense, I thought it was the VC." *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*
