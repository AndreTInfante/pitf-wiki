# Rabid mouse

**The rabid mouse** is a foaming, stiff-legged rodent from the bat-infested Pay n' Stay motel in Radford, Virginia, which bit Special Agent Daniel Webster and was speared by Kurt before being sent to Roanoke for testing.

## Description

The mouse is foaming, stiff-legged and jerky in its movements *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*. The table calls it a mouse and, more often later, "the rat."

## History

At about three in the morning in the [Pay n' Stay](../locations/pay-n-stay.md), the mouse crawls up [Daniel Webster's](../characters/daniel-webster.md) stomach. He flicks it off with his gun barrel (Kick Some Ass 9, a mixed result), and it bites him through his shirt on the nipple for 1 harm. [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md) rolls a 10 and spears it to the floor with his [spear gun](../items/spear-gun.md). [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md) pulls the spear from the floor, and Huell later picks the remains off it. *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*

Charm, the teenage clerk, arrives and admits a rodent problem: the motel's bat infestation gives the mice rabies. She offers the team their money back and says she does not sleep there herself. See [Charm](../characters/charm.md). *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*

In Radford Horrors 03 the team shares a patient room in the [Radford ER](../locations/radford-er.md). The doctor says, "Rabies, huh? All right, give me the rat," and Kurt hands over the harpoon with the rat still on it, in a plastic bag. There is no local DFA test, so the animal goes to Roanoke, which also supplies the HDCV vaccine. RIG begins at once: IV for everyone but Webster, who gets about twenty shots around his gumball-sized, hot nipple after a scrub and iodine soak. The treatment stops if the rat tests negative; otherwise the vaccine series is four shots, every Tuesday for four weeks. *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))* The treatment sets off [Andrew's alien dad](andrews-alien-dad.md) dream and his feverish reaction.

In Radford Horrors 04, Andre's recap and the team's own talk mention the mouse only as the reason everyone is on rabies treatment *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*.

## What remains unknown

The DFA result is pending as of Radford Horrors 03,. The episodes never report the result. *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*

- Kurt insists the harpoon is "a piece of sporting goods equipment" and "not a weapon" *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*.
