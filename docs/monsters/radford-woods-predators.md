# Radford woods predators

**The Radford woods predators** are the unidentified killers glimpsed in the cold open of Radford Horrors 01, which hunt the missing FBI team through the snowy woods: a shape that drains a man to a skeleton, a swooping flier and a mind-turned agent.

## Description

The cold open shows three separate things *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*:

- **The flying thing:** a flier that swoops over the fleeing agents and picks them off one by one from above. No clear details are seen.
- **The draining shape:** a dim shape hidden in the brush at the edge of the clearing. It lifts an agent and drains him of blood and fat "like a pouch of Capri Sun" until he is nearly skeletal, then tosses him like a ragdoll.
- **The mind-controlled agent:** a female agent aims at the shape, goes rigid, her eyes bloodshot and her nose bleeding, and turns her gun on a colleague. Whether the shape or something else compels her is not shown.

## Abilities

Flight, a draining attack that leaves the victim skeletal, and an apparent psychic compulsion. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*

## History

The attack takes place in the snowy night woods outside Radford, among the [hanging figures](hanging-figures.md). The agents wear yellow-lettered jackets and run with bouncing flashlights; one sobs. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*

After the credits, the players conclude that the agents were President Perot's handpicked follow-up team, who went AWOL in Radford (see [Missing FBI task force](../organizations/missing-fbi-task-force.md)). The cold open implies they were killed. Special Agent [Daniel Webster](../characters/daniel-webster.md) believes the team is dirty and covered something up in Radford. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*

## What remains unknown

It is never established whether the predators are related to the [Radford Moth](radford-moth.md), which Andre described as a UFO sighting that turned out to be "just moth," or what the shape and the flier are. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
