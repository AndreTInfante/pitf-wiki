# Lights and Eyes in the Woods

**Lights and Eyes in the Woods** are an unexplained low streak of light and a pair of blinking eyes that Andrew Howard sees around the Roulet house, never accounted for by the episodes.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Entity / phenomenon (unidentified) |
| **Aliases** | Streaking light; brilliant light; blinking eyes |
| **Region** | Woods around the [Roulet House](../locations/roulet-house.md), Exeter, Rhode Island |
| **First appearance** | [Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md) |

## Description

In [Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), while the group hammers at the floor of the weapons closet, Andrew steps outside and shouts "Can we just talk?" into the woods. He gets no response. As he heads back inside he glimpses a brilliant light streaking low through the trees, which is gone when he turns to look. He gives it the finger and says, "I hate the Greys. They suck so much." Soon afterwards the hammering stops, and the others find Kurt staring at the door, dizzy and disoriented, having lost a minute of time. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*

In the recap that opens [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md), Andre reminds the table that Andrew had just seen streaks of light in the woods. While Kurt is digging in the basement, Andrew keeps stepping out the front of the house to shout abuse at the Greys and then going back inside to throw up. On the third trip he sees eyes in the woods that blink at him several times and then disappear. He yells, "Get the fuck over here! At least talk! Don't be creepy!" and flips them off. *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

## What remains unknown

- Whether the light and the eyes belong to the [Roulet Intruder](roulet-intruder.md), the [Greys](greys.md) or something else.
- Whether the light caused Kurt's lost minute. The episodes only place the two in sequence.
- Whether any of it was real. The agents had drunk the house's [drugged water](../lore/drugged-water.md), and neither episode settles whether these sightings were hallucinations.
