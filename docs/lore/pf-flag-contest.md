# The Flag Contest

!!! note "Non-canon"
    The Flag Contest belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**The Flag Contest** is the national-holiday event at which children from disadvantaged communities show the president their designs for the tournament's logo, and the way [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) schemes to get the over-age [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) into the president's presence.

## Overview

After the safety briefing, Mirabeth asks whether the president's office has any child-outreach event she can sneak Derk into for publicity. Her Assess the Situation roll (a 9, with a -1 because there might not be one) reveals that a national holiday is associated with the tournament, on which kids from disadvantaged communities show off the promotional art they made for the logo. One design is picked, and a professional graphic artist turns it into the real logo. An old bridge buddy of Mirabeth's is on the committee. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## The scheme

On a video phone Mirabeth calls [her friend](../characters/pf-bridge-club-friend.md), claiming Derk is 14. The age limit for the event is 15. Her Pierce the Mask roll fails (3), so she gets only a place on the wait list and the address of a pre-event social in [the Capital](../locations/pf-capital.md). The friend is briefly confused when Mirabeth asks about peers for Derk, since the tournament's own entry limit is 17. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

Mirabeth sends Derk to the [Public Library](../locations/pf-public-library.md) to draw a flag, and then tears the result off the floor. At the community-center social, ten blocks away, the other children are at-risk teens. A bully insults Derk, and he does not understand it; a kind 14-year-old, Sarah, then befriends him and shows him her flag. Mirabeth meets [Addison](../characters/pf-addison.md) and her son and sells the [oil pyramid](../items/pf-oil-pyramid-kit.md). That night the friend calls to say a spot has opened up for Derk, who does not draw a better flag and keeps the stars and scorpions. The next morning, in a line of disadvantaged youths, he presents [his flag](../items/pf-derks-flag.md) to the [werewolf president](../characters/pf-werewolf-president.md). *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
