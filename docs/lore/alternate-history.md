# Alternate history

The **alternate history** of *PITF* is the campaign's version of 1992 and 1993: an early-1990s United States that looks like ours until the [Paranatural Incident Task Force](../organizations/paranatural-incident-task-force.md) starts to change it. The largest change is the 1992 election, which [Ross Perot](../characters/ross-perot.md) wins.

The politicians and celebrities on this page are fictional versions of real people, written and voiced by Andre as characters in a story. For dates, see the [timeline](timeline.md). For the supernatural side of the setting, see [the paranatural](the-paranatural.md).

## How the setting differs

The world does not split from ours at a single stated moment. When the campaign opens in May 1992 the Soviet Union has collapsed, [George H. W. Bush](../characters/george-h-w-bush.md) is president, and the agents carry pagers and stop to ask for directions ("There is no Google Maps"). The first difference is hidden: cryptids, parasites and stranger things are real, and a handful of people in government know it. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*

The public differences come later and are mostly the player characters' fault. A coastal town becomes the site of a nuclear incident, a surveillance scandal upends a presidential race, and two Utah towns are emptied. From the night of the election onward the table calls the result the "Perot-verse". *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md), [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

!!! quote
    **Zack (out of character):** "Have we entered the Perot-verse?" *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

## The Bush administration and the task force

In the premiere Andrew Howard is offered a new task force that "reports almost directly to the president": the chain of command runs from [Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md) to [Big Bill](../characters/big-bill.md) Nelson, to the deputy director Floyd Clark, to Bush. Andrew's old boss warns him that it "has boondoggle written all over it" and "smells like career suicide". *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md))*

Bush later tells Andrew the real reason. His son "has an interest in these things" and made him promise that if he won the election he would get to the bottom of it. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

The government's machinery for such things includes:

- **SKYHOOK**, the code word under which the team's reports are classified. *([Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md))*
- The [Foreign Influence Task Force](../organizations/foreign-influence-task-force.md), Big Bill's unit, which has the team because "alien visitations are foreign by definition". *([Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md))*
- The **Foreign Threat Investigation Task Force**, or "something like that": the bland name under which the team is described on television. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*
- The **Atolean Paranatural Index**, which ranks towns by UFO and cryptid sightings and disappearances per capita, and is used to pick cases. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md))*
- [UTAW](../organizations/utaw.md), the [CIA](../organizations/cia.md) branch where [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md) once did "psychic dream exercises", and [Black Site Omaha](../locations/black-site-omaha.md), a CIA prison five stories below the ocean surface. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md), [Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*

The [NSA](../organizations/nsa.md) is the "Puzzle Palace", which Andre explains is the CIA's name for it. *([Radford Horror 04](../episodes/radford-horror/04.md), [Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md), [Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*

## The 1992 election

In our history Bill Clinton won in 1992 and Perot took about 19 percent of the popular vote, a figure Zack looks up at the table. *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### The Georgia scandal

While the team is in [Dahlonega](../locations/dahlonega.md) hunting ape-men, Governor [Bill Clinton](../characters/bill-clinton.md) is in the same town. [Dick "The Biz" Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md) has secretly taken a job from his [KGB](../organizations/kgb.md) contacts to plant a [camera and microphone](../items/spy-camera-and-microphone.md) in a hotel room. Georgia state police then call a press conference to say that the FBI is in Dahlonega surveilling a presidential candidate, and that fingerprints from something recovered at the scene match an FBI agent. A reporter asks whether it is "the next Watergate". *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md), [Appalachia 08](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/08.md))*

!!! quote
    **Rita (as Andrew Howard):** "We're not surveilling Bill Clinton. We're surveilling dead Chewbacca." *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*

The arc's finale was never recorded, so the outcome is known only from the next session. Polaroids of Clinton, taken with a Russian camera, have become public. Bush's account is that a criminal with "a bunch of ape-men", [Jan Martense](../characters/jan-martense.md), was surveilling Clinton in the same town at the same time. The state police say they found the Biz's [night-vision goggles](../items/night-vision-goggles.md) in a rose bush, and he is "the most hated cop in America". The full story is on the [Clinton surveillance scandal](clinton-surveillance-scandal.md) page. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

### Two October surprises

Andre's summary of the alternate history is that Perot "just needed two October surprises", and that one event supplied both: the incumbent was caught spying on the challenger, and the pictures hurt the challenger too. He calls it "kind of Watergate and Monica Lewinsky at the same time". After the "double scandal" Perot polls above 45 percent and Bush clearly leads only in Texas. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

Bush expects to lose and reads the affair as a deliberate attack on the continuity of American leadership, perhaps by China, by "the remnants of the Soviets" or by "something else altogether, from somewhere else". This is his theory of [enemy action](enemy-action.md). *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

!!! quote
    **Andre (as Bush):** "There is coincidence, there is miracle, and there is enemy action. That is enemy action." *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

With the election close, the Bureau wants its version of the story out, and the price is putting the Biz on television. His appearance on *Late Night with [David Letterman](../characters/david-letterman.md)* opens the fourth arc, and in Utah he is recognized "from Leno". *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md), [Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*

### Election Day

Election Day, 3 November 1992, falls in the middle of the Utah case, with the phones down. When [Donald McManley](../characters/donald-mcmanley.md) next reaches the team the count is unfinished and Perot has taken Ohio. The team hears of his victory on the radio as they leave the ruins of the [community center](../locations/community-center.md). *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md), [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*

## The Perot presidency

Perot takes office in January 1993. As President-elect he tells Big Bill "in no uncertain terms" that he believes the task force is "a cover for Bush's weaponization of the FBI against his political enemies", which is how the Clinton affair looks from outside. He asks Big Bill to resign and disbands the task force on Inauguration Day. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

!!! quote
    **Dante (out of character):** "Guess what, Ross Perot? You're gonna have a rough first fucking day." *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*

A few months later he calls Andrew himself. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

!!! quote
    **Andre (as Perot):** "This is the President of the United States. I need you to come in. Something's happened in Virginia." *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

Other changes under Perot:

- **The FBI.** William Sessions is still Director in early 1993, with Floyd Clark rumored as his replacement. Perot fires him after reading the classified reports from Radford, which he finds unacceptable and Sessions stands by, and brings in [Rudy Giuliani](../characters/rudy-giuliani.md). Andre notes that Giuliani was then running for mayor of New York, an election he wins in our world. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md), [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md), [Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*
- **A new team.** Perot forms his own task force for "the insurgency incidents", as he sees the old team's cases. Its agents go missing in Radford (the [missing FBI task force](../organizations/missing-fbi-task-force.md)). *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- **Consultants.** The old team returns "strictly for informational purposes" under [Daniel Webster](../characters/daniel-webster.md), who helped with the rescue of Perot's employees from Iran and believes the old task force did Bush's political dirty work. Their orders are to find the missing agents and to establish an [acceptable fact pattern](acceptable-fact-pattern.md). *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md), [Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*

## Russia and Yeltsin

The Soviet Union is gone, but Marblehead locals still blame their offshore explosion on a "Russian bombing". The KGB survives as a network of fronts that reaches the Biz through a sweepstakes phone line and the [Moby-Dick book cipher](moby-dick-book-cipher.md). *([Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md), [Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md), [Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*

In January 1993 Russians take the Biz from an American hospital. He is held first at the abandoned [Vorkuta Corrective Labor Camp](../locations/vorkuta-corrective-labor-camp.md), is then flown to Moscow to meet [Boris Yeltsin](../characters/boris-yeltsin.md), and by April is living at the [Hotel Metropol](../locations/hotel-metropol.md) as Yeltsin's guest. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md), [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*

Yeltsin's Russia knows more than Washington admits to knowing:

- Soviet detectors recorded a [surge in the cosmic microwave background](cmb-surge.md) and traced it to the nuclear incident at Marblehead. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- Russian intelligence knows of the Marblehead worms and of the "people who were not people" in Utah, whom Yeltsin compares to Koschei the Deathless. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- Yeltsin shows the Biz a broken, half-melted [flashlight weapon](../items/flashlight-weapon.md) and says, "We are developing a test." *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- A [triangular craft](../items/triangular-craft.md) has crashed in Oryol Oblast. On the way there Yeltsin's convoy is [ambushed](../organizations/convoy-ambushers.md) by attackers speaking Chechen and Russian. *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md), [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*

!!! quote
    **Andre (as Yeltsin):** "I have faith in my military officers, but I do not have faith that my military officers are my military officers." *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*

## The official record

The public history of this world is a set of cover stories. After Marblehead Andre describes three versions of each case: the code-word report that goes to Big Bill, the one the rest of the government gets, and the one the public gets. *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))*

| Event | The official version | Source |
|---|---|---|
| [Radford](../locations/radford-virginia.md), May 1992 | The Whateley boy, a heroin connection to a Mexican [cartel](../organizations/cartel.md), a firefight at the old church and a fire | *([Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md), [Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))* |
| [Marblehead](../locations/marblehead.md), June 1992 | An extremist cult with nerve agent and a rogue ex-Soviet nuclear device, which attacked a nuclear plant when investigators arrived; the worms are played down (the [terrorist cover story](terrorist-cover-story.md)) | *([Summer Service 06](../episodes/summer-service/06.md), [Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md))* |
| Dahlonega, October 1992 | A criminal with ape-men who was surveilling a presidential candidate | *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))* |
| [Ballard](../locations/ballard-utah.md) and [Roosevelt](../locations/roosevelt-utah.md), November 1992 | A radical domestic insurgency: extremists under the community center who "went full Jonestown" when investigators showed up | *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))* |
| Radford, 1993 | Missing people are said to be in rehab in California (the [LA treatment cover story](la-treatment-cover-story.md)) | *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))* |

Andre stresses that Marblehead "was on the news for a while". The agents leave the spin to Big Bill. *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*

!!! quote
    **Dante (out of character):** "Yeah, I mean, we're the X-Files guys. We're not the cover-up guys." *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*

- **[Spud Hut](../organizations/spud-hut.md).** A baked-potato restaurant chain "founded in the area" of Dahlonega. *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*

- **[Keely Network](../organizations/keely-network.md).** The message boards through which Andrew hears about the Roulets. *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

- ***[Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md).*** An OVA that plays on hotel cable in Georgia. It is the campaign's non-canon one-shot. *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*

## Real people as characters

Besides the presidents and Yeltsin, the fiction borrows:

- **David Letterman**, who interviews the Biz on *Late Night*. *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*
- **[Robert Bigelow](../characters/robert-bigelow.md)**, the tenant of Sherman Ranch. When Zack recognizes the surname of "the Skinwalker Ranch guy", Dante says it is "probably intended to be the same guy". *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*
- **Rudy Giuliani** and **William Sessions** at the FBI. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- **Jay Leno** and **Howard Stern**, heard of but never met. *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*

Andre looked up Perot's voice just before playing him. Dante's verdict: "Why is Ross Perot a D&D goblin?" *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

## Popular culture

Most of the period detail is real, and the table checks it as it goes.

- **Television.** Watching *Roseanne* is Huell's "rock bottom". In Moscow the Biz watches *Los ricos también lloran* dubbed into Russian. The office has seven copies of *Close Encounters*, and the team watches it there on a slow Monday. *([Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md), [Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
- **Radio.** Art Bell plays in the Pay n' Stay office. "Baby Got Back" comes on in Radford after Andre checks what was charting in 1992, and "Radar Love" plays in two cold opens. *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md), [Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md), [Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
- **Food and shops.** The team eats at [McDonald's](../locations/mcdonalds.md) in Marblehead and Dahlonega, and the players look up which Happy Meal toys were on offer. Arby's, Taco Bell and Blockbuster Video all appear. *([Radford Horror 03](../episodes/radford-horror/03.md), [Summer Service 01](../episodes/summer-service/01.md), [Appalachia 01](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/01.md), [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*
- **Recent news.** Ruby Ridge, the Gulf War and Iran-Contra have happened as in our world, and Dante notes that the CIA's psychic-spy programs were real. The World Trade Center bombing and Waco still happen under Perot. *([Summer Service 04](../episodes/summer-service/04.md), [Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md), [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md), [Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

### Not yet

A running device is dramatic irony about things the characters cannot know. Huell shows off a World Trade Center postcard, and Kurt says the towers "will stand for a thousand years". The office's toy alien is named OJ, after "my favorite football athlete". During the Marblehead siege the table establishes that "Waco hasn't happened yet". *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Radford Horror 08](../episodes/radford-horror/08.md), [Summer Service 05](../episodes/summer-service/05.md))*

Andre rules anachronisms in or out on the spot: no *Beast Wars* toys, no purple Game Boy ("those won't be out for another four years"), and Tasers only just, in early 1993. *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md), [Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md), [Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*

## Trivia

- Crystal Pepsi is treated two ways. In the premiere Andre finds that it is advertised but "not available yet"; in Marblehead a month later it "had just come out". *([Radford Horror 01](../episodes/radford-horror/01.md), [Summer Service 03](../episodes/summer-service/03.md))*
- When the scandal breaks, one of the players asks who Bill Clinton was in 1992, and Andre reminds the table that it is October of that year. *([Appalachia 07](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/07.md))*
- Ezra jokes that the Russians who questioned the Biz about Clinton were "the Bill Clinton team", now sent to Siberia, and that a small "Ross Perot team" suddenly has all the budget. *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*
- Yeltsin's first question to the Biz is "Tell me everything you know about the humanists." Who they are has not been explained. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
- Speculating about where the Perot-verse goes next, Dante teases "wait till you fuckers find out about 417", and Andre offers that "there's, like, a bureaucratic fuck-up and the US accidentally nukes North Dakota". Nothing of the kind has happened in an episode. *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*
