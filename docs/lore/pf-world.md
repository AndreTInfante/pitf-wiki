# The World of Philosopher's Fist

!!! note "Non-canon"
    This world belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**The World of Philosopher's Fist** is a small, low-gravity planet that is a single country under a werewolf president, and is not the alternate-1990s United States of the main campaign (compare [Alternate history](alternate-history.md)).

## Overview

Andre describes the planet as significantly smaller than Earth, roughly half the size of the continental US, with roughly half a G of gravity. It is all one country, with a World Congress and a werewolf president, and the tone sits "somewhere between Japan and the US." *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

Aliens, androids or the like attack once or twice a decade, which is why the [World Martial Arts Tournament](../organizations/pf-world-martial-arts-tournament.md) exists as a pipeline of useful superhumans. Twice in the last forty years a champion has taken over the world and killed the president, so the tournament is both a defense and a threat. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## The president

The [werewolf president](../characters/pf-werewolf-president.md) turns into a wolf, and society treats it as normal. [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) thinks every person in charge is a werewolf, a running confusion. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Superhumans

Entrants fall into categories: the enormously strong, psychics, ki-blast users, wizards and pure martial artists. See [The Alien Faction](../organizations/pf-alien-faction.md) and [Pure Human Martial Artists](../organizations/pf-pure-human-martial-artists.md). Races and places named include the Gandekian (an alien race), Cantus (Derk's origin) and West City. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Daily life

- There are no cell phones, but video phones stand on practically every corner, with a map application that shows satellite photography.
- Online dating exists, and [Ovain](../characters/pf-ovain.md) announces that he is leaving "this planet and its Tinder."
- Social Security exists. [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) says "Social Security in this country should really be looked at"; Andre, as Colonel Sparago, replies, "That is literally part of the president's platform."
- Trade names in play include Omolene horse feed, Motel 6, McDonald's and this world's equivalent of *Jeopardy!*

*([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
