# PITF Wiki

Welcome to the fan wiki for ***PITF*: Paranatural Incident Task Force**, a *Monster of the Week* actual-play campaign run by Andre. The campaign is framed as a television show: sessions open cold, cut to credits, and follow a small FBI task force through an alternate early 1990s in which the strange cases are real and the cover stories are worse.

!!! warning "Spoilers"
    This wiki covers every recorded session and spoils all of them.
