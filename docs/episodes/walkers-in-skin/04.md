# The Walkers in Skin, Episode 04

A night patrol produces a coyote on two legs, a carbonized crow and two stoned teenagers; then the rental car vanishes straight upward and comes back flat in the next town.

## Synopsis

On the way to and during the 1 a.m. patrol, [Kurt Larson](../../characters/kurt-larson.md) is stalked by an [upright coyote](../../monsters/upright-coyotes.md), [Andrew Howard](../../characters/andrew-howard.md) finds a crow burned to charcoal in the pig pen, and the team frightens two Ballard teenagers out of the barn. When they get back, the [Plymouth Voyager](../../items/plymouth-voyager.md) is gone, with no tracks leaving. It turns up the next morning in [Roosevelt](../../locations/roosevelt-utah.md), a foot thick and fused into the road. The team acquires a loaner squad car called [Betsy](../../items/betsy.md), loses a jacket to the goose, wins the exhumation order through [Big Bill](../../characters/big-bill.md), and orders ground-penetrating radar and a bomb collar. *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

## Plot

### "Parlez-vous français?"

With an hour to go before the patrol, Kurt walks to [the big house](../../locations/big-house.md) to compare its instrument records with the substation pop and the car's sideways slide. A curt doorman says [Bigelow](../../characters/robert-bigelow.md) is asleep. Walking back, Kurt realizes he is being followed. A Preparedness roll (8 on the dice, 11 with Sharp) puts a wad of baloney in his pocket. He throws it and turns: a coyote is standing on two legs, eyes glinting. He asks it "Parlez-vous français?" as a verbal flashbang. It stares, then runs, dropping to all fours as it speeds up. [Nancy the goose](../../characters/nancy-the-goose.md) scuttles out and takes the baloney. Soon afterwards the Voyager's alarm goes off with nothing near it. *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

### The patrol

[Walt "Coyote" Murphy](../../characters/walt-murphy.md) collects them in his Jeep and falls in love with [Kurt's P90](../../items/kurts-p90.md). Andrew, watching the sky, sees something small drop into the pig pen. His Investigate a Mystery is a 9: it is a crow burned to a crisp, feathers crumbling, eyes burst, bones cracked by heat. It fell with no sound and no flash. Walt suggests ball lightning; Andrew cites the 1957 Levelland case, and half an hour of UFO talk moves Walt from suspicion to grudging respect. The crow goes into a dog-waste bag for [Donald McManley](../../characters/donald-mcmanley.md). *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

[The Biz](../../characters/dick-baxter.md) spots a figure with a glint near the barn. Kurt and [Huell Kendrick](../../characters/huell-kendrick.md) go in through a loose chain; Kurt's Read a Bad Situation (9) finds two extremely stoned teenagers behind the equipment. The glint was a holographic snap bracelet. Walt recognizes [Harrison](../../characters/harrison.md); the silent one is [Marshall Crichton](../../characters/minor-walkers.md). They came on a dare to look for ghosts or aliens. Harrison describes the tiny [Ballard High School](../../locations/ballard-high-school.md) (about sixty students, an eye-watering smell this semester). Kurt confiscates their terrible weed, comes back with good weed, and a Manipulate Someone (10) scares Harrison into taking it. Walt: "You guys are the weirdest fucking cops I've ever met." *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

### The vanishing car

Back at the [guest double-wide](../../locations/guest-double-wide.md) the Voyager is gone. One set of tire tracks leads in; none lead out. Huell's Investigate a Mystery is a 10: the car most likely went straight up, and the ground where it stood is about 200 degrees. Walt offers a junkyard magnet crane, a skyhook, a helicopter with a long cable, anything but "moon man talk". Around 3 a.m. Huell phones McManley ("I think there's aliens and shit. You should be over here") and hangs up. *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

About 10 a.m. Andrew's pager goes off. Hertz reports the car is blocking East Lagoon Street in Roosevelt. Andrew: "It got unabducted." At the big house the [Ponytail Lady](../../characters/ponytail-lady.md) has cued the camera tape ([Bigelow's recordings](../../items/bigelows-recordings.md)): a gray patch seems to rise, unconvincingly. Radar saw nothing, but the electromagnetic meters "went nuts", and the earlier substation pop and car slide match spikes too. Before they leave, Nancy gets into the double-wide and Huell shuts her inside (Act Under Pressure, a 12). The guard lends them dusty road bikes. Huell cannot ride one; double sixes on Act Under Pressure, even at -1, let him fake it for almost a mile and a half before he falls. This is the [vanishing car](../../lore/vanishing-car-incident.md). *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

### Roosevelt

The Voyager is pancaked about a foot thick and fused into a slight crater in the roadway. Officer Hernandez of the [Roosevelt Police Department](../../organizations/roosevelt-police-department.md) assumes vandals with a roller crusher and will check junkyards. He waives the tickets after a glance at Andrew's ID and arranges a loaner: Betsy, an old squad car that makes cartoon putt-putt noises. Five officers with jacks and two-by-fours cannot lever the wreck free. Huell's Help Out is double sixes again, a 15, and he lifts it with his back; ten to twenty percent of the car stays in the asphalt. Kurt's stilts, which he had "since Vietnam", went with the car; Betsy's trunk holds *Cats* costumes and a replacement pair. *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

### Goose, judge and fixers

The goose is still shut in the double-wide. In "Operation Get Goose Out" she drops onto Andrew's head from above the door, and [his pendant](../../items/andrews-pendant.md) repels the first strike. Kurt pries her off with the stilts (a 9), and the price is that she escapes with Andrew's jacket. The house is wrecked. *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

Andrew spends hours on the phone over the [exhumation](../../lore/exhumation-of-shelly-wilmarth.md). His Manipulate Someone (7) cannot move a judge with religious objections, so he calls Big Bill, who talks to the judge for twenty minutes; the judge calls back sounding shaken. The exhumation is set for the day after tomorrow. As a plan B, the Biz uses his Fixer move twice, rolling 12 both times. [Drebin](../../characters/drebin.md), a Salt Lake City dealer in search equipment and a friend of his ex-wife's brother (he is not named until [Walkers 05](05.md)), will rent them [ground-penetrating radar](../../items/ground-penetrating-radar.md) for about $800 and deliver it tomorrow. [Hooster](../../characters/hooster.md), a DC informant on parole and "criminal MacGyver", agrees to build a bomb collar so long as no dog is involved, supply thermite, and sell a net gun for the goose. His price is an invitation to the policeman's ball and help with parking tickets. Andrew retires to the bathroom so as not to be part of the conversation. *([Walkers 04](04.md))*

## Cold open and framing

A pale, morbidly obese man in a wife beater walks through the desert with a shovel to a trailer. He passes a large cross and a mirror in the entryway, takes something from the fridge and sits in a recliner. A human-shaped figure wrapped in something shiny is propped in the other chair. He lifts a plate of Jell-O to his chin, and fine gray tendrils spool out of his mouth onto it while his dead eyes watch the muted TV. "We cut to credits." The man is later identified as [Doug](../../characters/doug.md). *([Walkers 04](04.md))*
