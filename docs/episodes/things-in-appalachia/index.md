# The Things in Appalachia

**The Things in Appalachia** is the third arc of *PITF*, in which the task force goes to the north Georgia gold country to investigate disappearances and Bigfoot sightings and uncovers an illegal gold mine worked by ape-men beneath a potato restaurant chain's distribution plant.

## Overview

A few months after [Marblehead](../../locations/marblehead.md), the [Paranatural Incident Task Force](../../organizations/paranatural-incident-task-force.md) is handed a thin dossier: a missing boy, a missing woman and a run of Bigfoot sightings around Dahlonega, a gold-rush tourist town. The agents arrive to find the best hotel full of dental oncologists, a religious community called the [Covenant](../../organizations/covenant.md) living in a ghost town, a band of teenage monster hunters, and a potato restaurant, the [Spud Hut](../../organizations/spud-hut.md), whose trucks never stop running. *([Appalachia 01](01.md), [Appalachia 02](02.md))*

The case splits into three strands that take the whole arc to braid together. The first is a real predator: something pale and fast that climbs, hangs its leavings in trees, lures a man into a cave, takes a boy from a mine tour and mauls [Kurt Larson](../../characters/kurt-larson.md) in a hidden modern tunnel. The second is a hoax: clean giant footprints, a "Chewbacca" collected by a black sedan, and finally a dead ex-serviceman in a stolen costume, shot in the back of the head. The third is the Spud Hut itself, with its hundred-item menu, its savant counter man, its cipher of a secret menu, and its trucks hauling sacks of dirt. *([Appalachia 02](02.md), [Appalachia 03](03.md), [Appalachia 04](04.md), [Appalachia 06](06.md), [Appalachia 07](07.md))*

Running underneath is a private plot. [The Biz](../../characters/dick-baxter.md), on instructions from a [KGB](../../organizations/kgb.md) front, plants a camera in the hotel room next to the team's. The room's occupant turns out to matter a great deal: by the seventh session, state police are accusing the FBI of spying on presidential candidate [Bill Clinton](../../characters/bill-clinton.md), and the team is hiding from reporters while it works. *([Appalachia 01](01.md), [Appalachia 02](02.md), [Appalachia 07](07.md))*

The strands meet under the [Spud Hut plant](../../locations/spud-hut-regional-distribution-center.md). Its manager, Jan Martense, has spent a year and a half working ape-men in the old mines on ketamine, and his conspiracy staged the Bigfoot hoax to discredit sightings of the ones that escaped. He leaves the team to die on a sealed underground level. They get out through the mines, past a crack in the air that somebody else is using to pump gas into the tunnels. *([Appalachia 08](08.md), [Appalachia 09](09.md))*

Two sessions of the arc, including its finale, were played but never recorded, and several early threads (the Covenant, its river, the baptism of a vacuum salesman) are not picked up again in the recorded episodes. See [lost episodes](../../meta/lost-episodes.md).

## How it ended

The recorded story stops with the team hurt and loaded with gold in the woods outside the plant, and Jan driving away in a red pickup with a large animal crate. *([Appalachia 09](09.md))*

!!! note "Unrecorded session"
    The finale, [Appalachia 10](10.md), was played but never recorded. What follows is what the characters say about it in [Walkers 01](../walkers-in-skin/01.md).

The same day, the team caught up with Jan. He shot [Andrew Howard](../../characters/andrew-howard.md) in the face; Andrew survived and left hospital in about half the expected time. Kurt shot Jan, the Biz put a shotgun blast into him, and a falling ape-man broke his skull. He was taken alive and ended up in the CIA's [Black Site Omaha](../../locations/black-site-omaha.md). The Spud Hut facility exploded shortly after he fled it, which Kurt puts down to the gas that an unknown party piped into the mines. *([Walkers 01](../walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

A month later the [Clinton surveillance scandal](../../lore/clinton-surveillance-scandal.md) was still running. The official line blamed "Jan Martense, the illegal gold miner", but the state police had the Biz's [night-vision goggles](../../items/night-vision-goggles.md) from a rose bush, and he went on *Late Night with David Letterman* as "the most hated cop in America". At Omaha, Jan told Kurt that his ape-men were his own sons by [an ape woman he called Shelly](../../characters/shelly-ape-woman.md), whom he took five years earlier from the van of [a man with NSA identification](../../characters/dead-nsa-man.md) he had run over. *([Walkers 01](../walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

- Who opened [the crack in the air](../../lore/crack-in-the-air.md) and piped gas into the mine, and why.
- What Shelly was, and why a man with NSA identification had her.
- The fates of [Willow Greengrass](../../characters/willow-greengrass.md), [Gene Cartwright](../../characters/gene-cartwright.md) and [Frederick](../../characters/frederick.md), which are never confirmed on a surviving recording.
- The identity of the man whose lighter was marked "HF", and why Andrew walked to his remains in his sleep.
- What happened to [Jeremy](../../characters/jeremy.md) at his baptism, the Covenant's [land dispute](../../lore/land-dispute.md), and the Spud Hut runoff in its river.
- What became of the surviving ape-men, Sheriff Burke and the gold.
- Who shot [Troy Cleaver](../../characters/troy-cleaver.md). Kurt assumes it was Jan, and Jan does not deny it.
- Exactly what the Biz did on the night before [Appalachia 06](06.md), which was played in an unrecorded session and partly in private between Ezra and Andre.
- Whether the [cryptogram secret menu](../../items/cryptogram-secret-menu.md) was ever decoded.

## Trivia

- Episodes 04 and 07 survive only as single mixed audio tracks, so it is not always clear who is speaking in them.
- The in-fiction calendar drifts. In [Appalachia 01](01.md) the solo scenes fall on Sunday, October 3, the team is briefed in Washington on Monday the 4th, and it reaches Dahlonega at 7 p.m. on Tuesday the 5th, which makes [Appalachia 02](02.md) Wednesday the 6th and [Appalachia 03](03.md) and [04](04.md) Thursday the 7th; in [Appalachia 04](04.md) Andre believes it is Wednesday; in [Appalachia 07](07.md) he says the team arrived on October 4 and that it is now the 7th.
- The local newspaper is the *Dahlonega Times* in [Appalachia 01](01.md) and the *Gold Bug* in [Appalachia 06](06.md). The Bigfoot photograph is credited to "Charlie" Briggs in [Appalachia 02](02.md), but the photographer the team reaches in Appalachia 06 is Derek Edwards.
- Jan says in [Appalachia 08](08.md) that he grew up in East Germany, but the players call him Polish from then on. His name is heard as both "Jan" and "Yan"; Andre's explanation in [Appalachia 09](09.md) is that people in town do not say it correctly.
- The Claymore's toll is one ape-man dead in Andre's count at the end of [Appalachia 08](08.md) and two in the recap that opens [Appalachia 09](09.md).
