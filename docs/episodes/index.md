# Episode guide

This is the guide to every session of *PITF*, in the order the sessions were played: six case arcs and one non-canon one-shot. Thirty-seven canon sessions and the one-shot were recorded. Five more sessions were played but never recorded; they are listed in place, marked **unrecorded**, and are known only from what later episodes say about them (see [Lost episodes](../meta/lost-episodes.md)). For the television framing of the campaign, see [The show](../lore/the-show.md); for dates, see the [Timeline](../lore/timeline.md).

## The Radford Horror

In May 1992 four mismatched agents are assigned to the new [Paranatural Incident Task Force](../organizations/paranatural-incident-task-force.md) and sent to [Radford, Virginia](../locations/radford-virginia.md), where a teenager has vanished from a locked bedroom and a child says a spaceman took her. The case widens into missing women, missing dogs, a crooked psychiatrist and something blue that flies over the woods. It ends in a burning church with [the Radford Moth](../monsters/radford-moth.md), a cover story, and armed strangers who clean up behind the team.

| Episode | Summary |
|---|---|
| [Radford Horror 01](radford-horror/01.md) | The task force forms in a former D.C. barbershop and drives to Radford to look for Amanda Lucky, last seen by a four-year-old who blames a "spaceman." |
| [Radford Horror 02](radford-horror/02.md) | A second woman has walked into the woods; the team digs into the Whateley family's tragedies, the town's vanishing dogs and Dr. Lori's prescriptions. |
| [Radford Horror 03](radford-horror/03.md) | A day of stakeouts and a tailing white van ends with a blue flying object, armed "campers" and an NSA man whose flashlight sets Kurt on fire. |
| [Radford Horror 04](radford-horror/04.md) | Overnight the team recovers the NSA man's burned remains, the Biz burgles Dr. Lori's office, Phoebe Augustine vanishes, and a body turns up by the interstate. |
| [Radford Horror 05](radford-horror/05.md) | Amanda's autopsy shows she was crucified, sedated and partly eaten; the team works the crime scene, the patient files and a town meeting. |
| [Radford Horror 06](radford-horror/06.md) | A night stakeout brings a kidnapping, Huell carried into the air, a mummified body in the Whateley root cellar, and Wilbur Whateley gone from his bed. |

| [Radford Horror 08](radford-horror/08.md) | The team escapes the cleanup crew with a stolen poncho and flashlight weapon, closes Radford on a cover story, and is sent to Marblehead. |

## The Summer Service

In June 1992 the team goes to [Marblehead, Massachusetts](../locations/marblehead.md), to find an EPA and USGS field team that disappeared after an explosion out at sea. They find a town run by the [Sons of Adam](../organizations/sons-of-adam.md), a church whose communion wine is full of [worms](../monsters/worm-parasite.md), and a glowing creature offshore that the faithful take for an angel. The case ends at a nuclear power plant with a tactical nuclear weapon and a story about terrorists. The case opens in the last part of [Radford Horror 08](radford-horror/08.md).

| Episode | Summary |
|---|---|

| [Summer Service 02](summer-service/02.md) | **Unrecorded.** Known from later episodes: a dive in an experimental submarine to a stealth wreck, the first sight of the humming ring-shaped thing, and Kurt's nerve-agent exposure. |
| [Summer Service 03](summer-service/03.md) | Huell finds a blood-soaked house, the wine proves to be full of worms, and the lighthouse yields an interrogation site and the scientists' field journal. |
| [Summer Service 04](summer-service/04.md) | The team sends for Ivermectin, acquires a Davy Crockett through Kurt's old CIA contact, and faces a Board of Selectmen that meets under the seal of the Sons of Adam. |
| [Summer Service 05](summer-service/05.md) | Arnold Weathers and his dog are found as worm-filled hosts and destroyed; police and church members besiege the hotel and the team escapes by a bathroom window. |
| [Summer Service 05.5](summer-service/05-5.md) | **Unrecorded.** Known from remarks in Summer Service 06: the scientists are found alive, the team steals a car, and the creature comes ashore behind a cult convoy. |

## The Things in Appalachia

In October 1992, with the Marblehead fallout still before Congress, the team is sent to [Dahlonega, Georgia](../locations/dahlonega.md), over two disappearances and a run of Bigfoot sightings. The trail runs past a river cult and a hoaxer in a Chewbacca costume to the [Spud Hut](../organizations/spud-hut.md) plant, where [ape-men](../monsters/ape-men.md) are mining gold underground. Meanwhile the Biz's side job for the KGB turns into a national scandal about the FBI spying on Bill Clinton. The finale was never recorded. The non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable* was played between the first and second sessions of this arc and is listed at that point.

In the recap that opens [Appalachia 02](things-in-appalachia/02.md), Andre decided retroactively that it was an OVA on the Smith Hotel's cable television, which Andrew half remembers from turning the set on to fall asleep.

## The Walkers in Skin

In the days before the 1992 election the team follows a report of a dead woman seen alive to [Sherman Ranch](../locations/sherman-ranch.md) in Utah. The ranch's lights, vanishing cars and upright coyotes give way to something worse in the towns nearby: people are being replaced by [skinwalkers](../monsters/skinwalkers.md). Andrew Howard is among those replaced. The arc ends with the National Guard, a portal under a community center, and Ross Perot winning the presidency.

| Episode | Summary |
|---|---|

| [Walkers 02](walkers-in-skin/02.md) | The team settles into a double-wide on the ranch and meets Alberta, Walt, Robert Bigelow and the people of Ballard. |
| [Walkers 03](walkers-in-skin/03.md) | A dusk electrical event kills and moves the car; the team starts the exhumation request and visits the grave, the substation and the community center site. |
| [Walkers 04](walkers-in-skin/04.md) | On night patrol a coyote walks upright and the rental car vanishes straight up, to be found fused into a street in Roosevelt. |
| [Walkers 05](walkers-in-skin/05.md) | A night event brings shared hallucinations, levitation and a ring of light Bigelow calls the Black Sun; radar finds a vast cavern under the ranch. |
| [Walkers 06](walkers-in-skin/06.md) | Ballard is emptying out, the sheriff flees the state, Huell is bitten, and Andrew gets a note summoning him alone to the school gym. |

## The Roulet Estate

On Inauguration Day 1993, with President Perot about to disband the task force, the team wakes hungover and without memories in an abandoned house in Rhode Island, alongside Diane and a monk they seem to have kidnapped. Something that looks like a [Grey](../monsters/greys.md) is shooting at them with a Soviet rifle. The answer is more human than expected, and the arc closes with the team scattered and the Biz in Russian hands.

Arc page: [The Roulet Estate](roulet-estate/index.md)

| Episode | Summary |
|---|---|
| [Roulet Estate 01](roulet-estate/01.md) | After flashbacks to Utah and Christmas Eve, the team wakes stranded at 118 Benefit Street, where a grey figure with a sniper rifle wounds Huell. |
| [Roulet Estate 02](roulet-estate/02.md) | Two bodies, a stolen car, a hidden attic lair occupied for a year or more, and the discovery that the house's water is drugged. |
| [Roulet Estate 03](roulet-estate/03.md) | A hostage siege at a pharmacy unmasks the "alien" as a man in a latex mask; months later the team is disbanded and Perot phones Andrew. |

## The Radford Horrors

In April 1993 Perot calls the former task force back as consultants under a by-the-book new agent, [Daniel Webster](../characters/daniel-webster.md), to find an FBI team that vanished in Radford. The town has new disappearances, a fenced site owned by the [Safe Environment Fund](../organizations/safe-environment-fund.md), and traces of more moths. In Russia the Biz is the guest and prisoner of Boris Yeltsin. This is the latest arc, and its story is unfinished as of episode 04.

Arc page: [The Radford Horrors](radford-horrors/index.md)

| Episode | Summary |
|---|---|
| [Radford Horrors 01](radford-horrors/01.md) | The Biz is questioned by Yeltsin in Moscow while the others search his apartment and are placed under Webster by the new FBI director, Giuliani. |
| [Radford Horrors 02](radford-horrors/02.md) | Back in Radford: an IRS raid, the missing agents' tents, a clash at the Safe Environment Fund fence in which Andrew is shot, and a rabid mouse. |
| [Radford Horrors 03](radford-horrors/03.md) | The Biz saves Yeltsin from a convoy ambush; in Radford the team takes rabies shots and investigates an abduction at the Pinball Emporium. |
| [Radford Horrors 04](radford-horrors/04.md) | A survivor of a crashed triangular craft says "not aliens, the opposite of aliens"; Andrew wakes Wilbur Whateley, who says the moth was pregnant. |

## Notes on numbering and dates

- Episode numbers follow each arc's own count, so the unrecorded sessions keep their slots: Summer Service 02, Appalachia 05 and Appalachia 10. Two more fell between numbered sessions and are listed as Summer Service 05.5 and Walkers 08.5.
- The Marblehead case is handed to the team at the end of [Radford Horror 08](radford-horror/08.md), and [Walkers 01](walkers-in-skin/01.md) is mostly the epilogue of the Georgia case.
- The one-shot has no episode number and sits outside every arc.
- Andre dates the opening of The Radford Horrors to "early April '93." *([Radford Horrors 01](radford-horrors/01.md))*
