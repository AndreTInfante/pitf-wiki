## Synopsis

[The Biz](../../characters/dick-baxter.md) is flown with a bandaged [Boris Yeltsin](../../characters/boris-yeltsin.md) to a [triangular craft](../../items/triangular-craft.md) buried in a Russian farm field, where a [bisected survivor](../../monsters/crash-survivor.md) answers three questions before passing out. In Radford, [Kurt Larson](../../characters/kurt-larson.md) obtains the diner's [security tape](../../items/diner-security-tape.md), which shows a [gray van crew](../../organizations/gray-van-crew.md) removing something non-human from the [Douglas Pinball Emporium](../../locations/douglas-pinball-emporium.md). The team drives to Roanoke, where [Andrew Howard](../../characters/andrew-howard.md) lays a hand on the catatonic [Wilbur Whateley](../../characters/wilbur-whateley.md) and heals him enough to talk about the moth. They end the day camped beside the missing agents' tents, with [Daniel Webster](../../characters/daniel-webster.md) quietly adding up everything strange about Andrew. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

## Plot

### The opposite of aliens

The Biz wakes locked in a hospital room on an evacuated floor, four guards with machine guns outside. At dawn a guard he knows from the hotel says, "Mr. Baxter, the President would like to see you." Yeltsin, sutured at the head and torso, thanks him and praises the "impressive stunt driving." The Biz explains that driving drunk was "like training with a weight vest on." Russian intelligence thinks the attack was "opportunistic and fairly half-assed." *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

That night a helicopter carries Yeltsin's hospital bed and the Biz to the crash site, and the Biz helps push the bed through an airlock. Under arc lamps, behind double-walled plastic, sits a partly excavated triangular craft a little smaller than the space shuttle, which annihilated a farmhouse when it came down. In the tent are two beds joined by tubes carrying blood, urine and other fluids. One holds a shirtless Russian soldier. The other holds "about 55% of a guy": one arm gone, nothing below the navel, hairless, with the wrong teeth and jaw and eyes set too far apart. The only thing he reminds the Biz of is the subtle change that came over Andrew Howard after he died, "but much more so." *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

Doctors wake him with a syringe, and Yeltsin asks his questions in Russian, then English. Why are you here? "Survey." Why are your people here, the aliens? "Not aliens. The opposite of aliens." What do you want? "[Servitors](../../monsters/servitors.md)... need resources." He passes out, and the doctors warn that another dose might kill him. In private, the Biz decides against feigning knowledge. These are not the aliens he has dealt with, he says; he has seen resources go through portals, but it "presented differently," and he has never heard of servitors. Yeltsin notes that the survivor accepts O-negative blood, and asks why that would work on an alien. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

### The tape

Kurt volunteers to face [the diner waitress](../../characters/sheila.md), who has a bone to pick about her bathroom. A Manipulate Someone 11, an apology for [Huell](../../characters/huell-kendrick.md) and the promise of a hundred-dollar tip get him her camera footage. At about 4 or 5 a.m. a gray van pulls up at the emporium. Five men get out, one with a long brown ponytail and the rest with military haircuts. A shadow that is not human moves inside the front window. One man opens the lock, there is a scuffle, three men carry out two bags, others carry equipment, and the van peels away. Kurt's Investigate a Mystery (10) reads them as an organized paramilitary cleanup team, not military, and the ponytailed man as a boss playing soldier. The shadow seemed to be trying to get out as the door opened. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

The waitress says gray vans began appearing a couple of months ago, around the "right to roam" fuss over the woods and the arrival of the [Safe Environment Fund](../../organizations/safe-environment-fund.md). At the emporium, Kurt's second roll (11) finds the way in: an unlocked, screenless office window on the alley, with a compost smell like the moth's and a second dollop of [opalescent slime](../../items/pearlescent-goo.md) above it. The Smart-car-sized [moth](../../monsters/radford-moth.md) could never have fit. His theory is that [a juvenile moth or a thrall](../../monsters/emporium-intruder.md) got inside, the crew came for it, and [Elizabeth George](../../characters/elizabeth-george.md) was taken because she saw. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

Huell phones [Donald McManley](../../characters/donald-mcmanley.md): "Get down here, we've got goo." McManley asks whether there is a face this time, hears "Not yet," says "Small fucking mercies," and promises to arrive tomorrow in his RV. At Blockbuster Video the clerk [Harold](../../characters/harold.md) lets the team view and copy the tape in the back room. Harold saw a ponytailed man drag a homeless man into a van a month and a half or two months ago. Kurt walks twenty minutes into the woods and hides the copy in a hollow tree. Sheriff [Molly Underhill](../../characters/molly-underhill.md), reached by phone, gives the homeless man a name, [Billy Thornton](../../characters/billy-thornton.md); he cannot be found. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

Andrew, sweating and light-sensitive, refuses the ibuprofen and steroid syringes from the ER and instead mixes blood into lemon-lime Gatorade in front of Webster ("This is my protein mix... This is meds."). He feels better at once. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

### Giuliani's deadline

Webster reports to Director [Rudy Giuliani](../../characters/rudy-giuliani.md). He sees no link to "political wetwork," he says, and the team talks openly of "Mothman, Bigfoot, aliens." Giuliani thinks it reckless to run lunatics in the field and wants the [missing agents](../../organizations/missing-fbi-task-force.md) found within 24 hours, dead or alive. Webster passes on what the team suspects: the vans came with the Fund, and the missing people's letters all cite [an addiction center in Los Angeles](../../lore/la-treatment-cover-story.md) they could not afford. Giuliani already has a man looking at the Fund and assigns a researcher. On a Manipulate Someone 10 he is disappointed but still trusts Webster. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

### Wilbur

The team drives most of the day to Roanoke and reaches the mental hospital an hour before closing. A nurse warns them: no touching. Wilbur Whateley sits in a wheelchair facing the window in a room decorated with his bugs. Kurt's first approach (9, at -2) earns a flicker in the eye. He then holds the goo sample under Wilbur's nose, and Wilbur's muscles stiffen, his eyes go waxy and his fingers clamp the chair. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

Andrew uses magic and rolls a 13. He senses that Wilbur is not being controlled now, but that his mind is badly damaged from years of being "ridden shotgun" and of faking catatonia. He chooses to heal one harm and puts a hand on Wilbur's shoulder. Wilbur wakes: "Where's my brother?" He recognizes the agents "from the church." He dreamed of "drowning in blue," and says something was "wearing" him. He found an oversized *Morpho menelaus* caterpillar in the woods, a species that does not belong in Virginia. Kurt's follow-up roll is a 7, and Wilbur begins to panic at what he must not remember: "like the ocean... like a mother... in the basement... It was pregnant. It needed to breathe... it needed hosts, like a parasitic wasp. Those women--". As the nurse ushers them out he adds, "I found it under a white oak." *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

### The campsite

Back in Radford with an hour before the shops close, the agents buy tents and food and pitch camp at the [Sportsman RV Campground](../../locations/sportsman-rv-campground.md) beside the missing team's tents, which they leave undisturbed as evidence. Kurt's tent goes up on a 13; Huell's, on a 9, stands at a twenty-degree tilt with two rods left over. Over hot dogs Kurt tells Webster the story of [the Spaceman](../../monsters/spaceman.md): townspeople reported a NASA astronaut, and it turned out that Wilbur's space-enthusiast brother had built an accurate astronaut costume in which people were being kidnapped. A real explanation for a crazy report makes Webster feel better. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

Webster's Sharp 12 then assembles the rest: [Andrew's bag](../../items/andrews-satchel.md) produces more than it could hold, Andrew tore barbed wire and snaps branches that should not snap, Webster is increasingly sure he saw him shot, and he keeps drinking blood. Webster says nothing. Andrew asks how many young a moth has; Kurt, on a Sharp 10, recalls from Animal Planet that the answer is [40 to 500 eggs](../../lore/moth-life-cycle.md). Kurt settles down to sleep tied into a tree, a habit from Vietnam, and Andrew takes him by the belt and lifts him up there one-handed while Webster watches. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

## Revelations

- The beings behind the crashed craft call themselves "the opposite of aliens," are here on "survey," and say servitors need resources. Their survivor can take human O-negative blood. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- The survivor resembles a stronger version of whatever changed Andrew after his death. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- A paramilitary crew in gray vans is collecting moth-related creatures and cleaning up after them. The same ponytailed man abducted Billy Thornton, which supports his story. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- Wilbur was the moth's thrall, not a killer by choice. The moth was pregnant, used women as hosts "like a parasitic wasp," and began as a caterpillar Wilbur found under a white oak. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- A moth can lay hundreds of eggs. Andrew: "We're gonna be doing this for the rest of our careers." *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- Webster now suspects Andrew is something other than human. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

## Continuity

- Wilbur's account revisits the finale of [The Radford Horror](../radford-horror/index.md): the church fire, the moth that "wore" him, and the kidnappings carried out in a homemade spacesuit.
- McManley's "face" refers to the [folded face](../../items/folded-face.md) of [The Things in Appalachia](../things-in-appalachia/index.md), a case that makes Webster see his career flash before his eyes.
- The survivor's "Survey" and the Biz's mention of resources going through portals are followed up at [The paranatural](../../lore/the-paranatural.md).
- Andrew's death and replacement in Utah is recalled as "Dummy Andrew"; see [Andrew Howard (duplicate)](../../characters/andrew-howard-duplicate.md).
- This is the most recent episode. Open questions are collected at [Unresolved mysteries](../../lore/unresolved-mysteries.md).
- In the opening recap Dante remembers Andrew being shot "in, like, the face or something" at the fence, and Andre agrees. In [Radford Horrors 02](02.md) the bullet stopped an inch from his chest. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- Andre's recap calls the missing arcade worker Lisbeth George; Douglas introduced her as Elizabeth George in [Radford Horrors 03](03.md). *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- Andre says Wilbur spent "like, five years" being ridden by the moth and pretending to be catatonic, which reaches back well before the first Radford case; Kurt afterwards puts the coma at "a couple years." Andre also confirms that Wilbur is "the spaceman kid" and not his space-obsessed brother. *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
- The syringes Andrew turns down are the ibuprofen and corticosteroids the ER issued to Kurt in [Radford Horrors 03](03.md). *([Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*
