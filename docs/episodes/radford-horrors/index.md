# The Radford Horrors

**The Radford Horrors** is the sixth and current canon arc of *PITF*, in which the disbanded task force is sent back to the town of its first case as consultants to a suspicious new agent, while the Biz sits out the story as Boris Yeltsin's prisoner and adviser in Russia.

## Overview

Three months after Rhode Island, the [Paranatural Incident Task Force](../../organizations/paranatural-incident-task-force.md) no longer exists. President [Ross Perot](../../characters/ross-perot.md) reads the file on its first case, which says a giant insect was responsible, fires the FBI Director for standing by it, and installs [Rudy Giuliani](../../characters/rudy-giuliani.md). Perot's own handpicked team of five career agents has meanwhile vanished in the Radford woods; the arc's first cold open shows them being hunted. Giuliani recalls [Andrew Howard](../../characters/andrew-howard.md), [Kurt Larson](../../characters/kurt-larson.md) and [Huell Kendrick](../../characters/huell-kendrick.md) as consultants under Special Agent [Daniel Webster](../../characters/daniel-webster.md), with two orders: find the missing agents, and "establish an [acceptable fact pattern](../../lore/acceptable-fact-pattern.md)" for the original investigation. *([Radford Horrors 01](01.md), [Radford Horrors 02](02.md))*

The arc inverts the team's usual position. They are no longer in charge, and their new boss, Ezra's second character, believes they were George H. W. Bush's political dirty-tricks squad and that "alien stuff" is a cover. Much of the comedy comes from Webster recording everything, counting how many people Kurt has skinned, and writing "Andrew Howard is a vampire?" in his notebook, and much of the tension from his slow recognition that the truth is stranger than his theory. *([Radford Horrors 02](02.md), [Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

Radford itself has changed. [Molly Underhill](../../characters/molly-underhill.md) is sheriff, [Douglas Douglas](../../characters/douglas-douglas.md) owns a pinball arcade, and the woods are being bought and fenced by the Safe Environment Fund, which has armed guards, lawyers and a talent for bleaching crime scenes. More townspeople are missing, including [Phoebe Augustine](../../characters/phoebe-augustine.md), and each absence is explained by a letter about [treatment in Los Angeles](../../lore/la-treatment-cover-story.md). A slime that smells like the dead moth turns up in the trees and above an arcade window, and [Wilbur Whateley](../../characters/wilbur-whateley.md), woken from catatonia by Andrew, says the moth was pregnant. *([Radford Horrors 02](02.md), [Radford Horrors 03](03.md), [Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

A second storyline follows [the Biz](../../characters/dick-baxter.md), a prisoner in Russia since January and now held in comfort at the [Hotel Metropol](../../locations/hotel-metropol.md). [Boris Yeltsin](../../characters/boris-yeltsin.md), who fears his own officers have been replaced, questions him about every case the team has worked. When the presidential convoy is ambushed on the way to a crash site, the Biz saves Yeltsin's life and loses the sight of an eye. At the site, a dying survivor of a triangular craft says his people are "the opposite of aliens." *([Radford Horrors 01](01.md), [Radford Horrors 03](03.md), [Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

## Where it stands

The arc has not ended. As of [Radford Horrors 04](04.md), the most recent episode:

- The team is camped beside the missing agents' tents, waiting for McManley and his RV, with a 24-hour deadline from Giuliani to find the agents dead or alive.
- A copy of the diner tape showing the gray van raid is hidden in a hollow tree, and two samples of goo await analysis.
- Wilbur Whateley is awake and frightened in Roanoke.
- Andrew is on rabies treatment that his body reacts badly to and is managing with blood; Webster has noticed the bag, the strength, the bullet and the blood, and has said nothing.
- A Bivens lawsuit over the fence is pending against the agents.
- The Biz is in Russia, blind in one eye, three Luck from Doomed, and in Yeltsin's confidence.

*([Radford Horrors 03](03.md), [Radford Horrors 04](04.md))*

- Who attacked Yeltsin's convoy, where the Russians got a [flashlight weapon](../../items/flashlight-weapon.md), and how they know so much about American cases. *([Radford Horrors 01](01.md), [Radford Horrors 03](03.md))*

- What "acceptable fact pattern" Webster will end up filing. *([Radford Horrors 01](01.md))*

## Trivia

- Andre dates the arc to "early April '93" in [Radford Horrors 01](01.md), three months after the Biz was taken in January.
- The rabies course is four doses over two weeks in [Radford Horrors 02](02.md) and one every Tuesday for four weeks in [Radford Horrors 03](03.md). The animal that bit Webster is a mouse in the first and "the rat" from the second onward.
- The missing arcade worker is introduced as Elizabeth George in [Radford Horrors 03](03.md); Andre's recap in [Radford Horrors 04](04.md) calls her Lisbeth.
- Andrew was shot in the chest at the fence in [Radford Horrors 02](02.md); the recap in [Radford Horrors 04](04.md) loosely remembers it as "the face or something."
- Yeltsin is named in [Radford Horrors 01](01.md) and [Radford Horrors 04](04.md) but is only "the president" throughout [Radford Horrors 03](03.md).
