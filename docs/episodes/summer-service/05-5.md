# The Summer Service, Episode 05.5

A session of [The Summer Service](index.md) was played between the hotel siege and the finale but never recorded; in it the team found the missing scientists alive, lost its FBI car, stole another, and ended up behind a convoy of the infected on a flooded freeway with the angel thing coming ashore after them.

!!! note "Unrecorded session"
    This session was played but never recorded. It has no number of its own; "05.5" marks its place between [Summer Service 05](05.md) and [Summer Service 06](06.md). No quotes or roll results survive, and Summer Service 06 gives no recap. Everything below is what that episode establishes in passing.

## Where the recordings break off and resume

[Summer Service 05](05.md) ends before dawn on the road toward Salem. The team has escaped the hotel in [Donald McManley](../../characters/donald-mcmanley.md)'s RV, switched to [the Biz's Buick](../../items/biz-buick-regal.md) behind the abandoned gas station, and picked up McManley's assistant with the [ivermectin](../../items/ivermectin.md); the players put off deciding whether to take it. *([Summer Service 05](05.md))*

[Summer Service 06](06.md) opens in the afternoon, in the storm, on [the flooded freeway](../../locations/flooded-freeway-and-marsh.md). The team is in a stolen car behind a slow convoy of the infected, and the angel thing is "making its way towards you, trenching out the ground". *([Summer Service 06](06.md))*

## What Summer Service 06 establishes

- **The scientists were found alive.** [Andrew Howard](../../characters/andrew-howard.md) tells the radio contact, "we found our research guys. They were alive." Testifying to a House subcommittee a few months later, Kurt adds that the [survey team](../../organizations/epa-usgs-survey-team.md) "had been kidnapped and held hostage up until that point". *([Summer Service 06](06.md), [Appalachia 01](../things-in-appalachia/01.md))*
- **The team lost its FBI car.** It turns up near the back of the convoy with [Jester](../../characters/jester.md) at the wheel and [Kurt Larson](../../characters/kurt-larson.md)'s gear still inside. Andrew's account is simply, "they carjacked us."
- **The team stole a car.** Andre reminds the players "that you stole this car", and that they are glad they "didn't take the Biz's Buick" into wheel-well-high water. The [Davy Crockett](../../items/davy-crockett.md), mounted on the Buick in [Summer Service 04](04.md), is with them in the stolen car.
- **The infected formed a convoy** heading for [the nuclear power plant](../../locations/nuclear-power-plant.md), and the team followed it.
- **The angel thing came ashore.** The [creature](../../monsters/angel-thing.md) left the sea and came up the freeway after the team. The team set off a claymore on a truck; Kurt says they were "trying to slow down whatever the hell it is". The sonar operator in the finale's cold open hears this as a small explosion, "maybe like a grenade", just after the creature "went on land".
- **Huell took a dewormer.** Early in the finale Andre tells Zack that "the deworming agent is kicking in" for [Huell Kendrick](../../characters/huell-kendrick.md), and the worm bonus comes off his sheet.
- **The team had fought worm bags.** Asked whether they have fought [those things](../../monsters/worm-bags.md), Kurt answers, "Yeah, pretty gnarly."
- **The lab techs stayed behind.** Dante checks that "we left the lab tech guys there, right, with the RV", and Andre confirms it.
- **The Guard had been called.** The National Guard sergeant on the radio is looking for "the FBI agents who called us out".

*([Summer Service 06](06.md))*

The [radio contact](../../characters/radio-contact.md) opens the finale by apologizing for "my commanding officer's rudeness earlier". The only such exchange in a recorded episode is the voice that says "We're the goddamn Marines. Get off the line" in Summer Service 05; whether the two sides spoke again in the unrecorded session is not established. *([Summer Service 05](05.md), [Summer Service 06](06.md))*
