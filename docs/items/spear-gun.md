# Kurt's spear gun

**Kurt's spear gun** is a single-shot, cabled harpoon gun that Kurt carries as "sporting goods equipment." It killed the rabid mouse, alarmed airport security and became a talking point in the lawsuit that followed the SEF fence incident.

## Description

The spear gun is a pressurized, single-shot weapon whose spears are attached to spools of wire cable. Kurt says the cable is shark-resistant, with good tensile strength, and defends the gun's "penetration capability." Once a spear is fired, the spool must be detached or another spear loaded and the gun re-pressurized, which Kurt admitted makes it a poor choice for anything like taking over a plane. He sleeps with it *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*.

## Properties

Kurt brought the spear gun to the Radford woods alongside his P90, a grenade, caltrops and about 30 gallons of pesticide. Daniel Webster, who asked why Kurt would bring a spear gun rather than a rifle, grew increasingly convinced Kurt would shoot him with it to make a point. Kurt, for his part, reflected that he regretted never having brought a spear gun into an interrogation room, since it clearly "has presence" *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*.

## History

### Airport security

At the airport, the spear gun was held up because the weapons-transport paperwork had been misplaced and Kurt had no FBI badge. His Act Under Pressure roll to find a coworker to vouch for him failed (3, or 5 with a bonus), and the airport was in chaos because of a strike. A Manipulate Someone roll (9, mixed) succeeded when he pulled down his collar to show where a Vietnamese spear had pierced his shoulder: "I bled for this country. I continue to bleed for this country." The screener told him to pack the spear separately *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*. On the plane, Kurt complained to Andrew about how hard it would be to hijack a plane with a spear gun.

### The rabid mouse

At the Pay n' Stay at about 3 a.m., a foaming, stiff-legged mouse crawled up Webster's stomach and bit him on the nipple. Kurt woke, aimed and fired in one motion, and a Kick Some Ass roll speared the mouse through the torso, nailing it to the floor. Andrew pulled the spear out of the floor, and the clerk, [Charm](../characters/charm.md), admitted the motel had been promised the rodents were exterminated *([Radford Horrors 02](../episodes/radford-horrors/02.md))*. See [the rabid mouse](../monsters/rabid-mouse.md).

In Radford Horrors 03, the team shared a hospital room for rabies treatment. When the doctor said "All right, give me the rat," Kurt extended the harpoon with the mouse still on it, wrapped in a plastic bag. It was to be tested in Roanoke, since the local hospital lacked the supplies; the team would need a four-vaccine series unless the result came back negative *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*.

### The lawsuit

The lawyers for the Safe Environment Fund raised the spear gun in the dispute that became the [Bivens lawsuit](../lore/bivens-lawsuit.md). The guard who had tased Webster claimed he did not believe the FBI would be carrying one, which was why he fired, and also had a hearing impairment. Webster pointed out that Kurt had not shot anyone with it. Andrew objected that it was technically a harpoon, not a spear gun *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*.

    "If they ask, I had a piece of sporting goods equipment on me. Not a weapon: sporting goods equipment, purchased from a sporting goods store." *(Dante (as Kurt), [Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*
