# Alberta's box

**Alberta's box** is the carton of documents and tapes that [Alberta Wilmarth](../characters/alberta-wilmarth.md) leaves with [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md) when she asks him to force an exhumation of her dead mother, and which sets *The Walkers in Skin* in motion.

## Description

Alberta arrives at Andrew's apartment in Washington, D.C. holding a big box of documents and tapes, having eaten nothing but corn nuts for three days on the road. She shows him her mother [Shelly Wilmarth](../characters/shelly-wilmarth.md)'s yearbook photo, a family photo from a big reunion five or six years earlier, an obituary clipping, and a VHS copy of local news footage of a protest about the community center *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*.

## Properties

The box is evidence rather than anything paranormal in itself. The obituary says Shelly died in the winter of 1990, and Alberta says she died two years earlier, of a heart attack while out with the cattle on the family ranch. Yet the tape, paused just before a cut, seems to show her alive. Alberta also says the tenant of the ranch (the "space guy", later identified as [Robert Bigelow](../characters/robert-bigelow.md)) has seen Shelly walking around the edges of the property at night. In her pitch Andre (as Alberta) insists that "she's not a ghost" and "She's just alive again." *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

## History

**The pitch.** Rita (as Andrew) rules out a secret twin, since Alberta has only brothers, and says doppelgangers copy children rather than older women; then, hearing that Shelly died alone, he puts "doppelganger" back on the list and delivers a long lecture. Alberta describes the ranch as plagued by boiled cattle, missing pets, UFO lights and Navy radiation investigators, and leaves *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*. Andrew heats her some leftover chicken and noodles.

**Follow-up.** Big Bill tells Andrew the task force is welcome to look into it and that the FBI will pay for the plane tickets, and the team flies to Utah. In a recap for the Biz's player, [Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md) restates that the client said her mother had died on the ranch and then showed up in TV coverage of a protest in town. The team's goal becomes the [exhumation](../lore/exhumation-of-shelly-wilmarth.md). In [Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md) Rita asks when Shelly died, and Andre answers about two months ago, which makes the TV appearance a few weeks old and possibly part of an escalation. That disagrees with Alberta's two years in Walkers 01.

**Where the footage matters.** The tape shows the protest outside the community center, the same project that [Maggie](../characters/maggie.md) later says was built on bulldozed Pueblo ruins despite a protest *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*.
