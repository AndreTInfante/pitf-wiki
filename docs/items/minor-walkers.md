# Minor items and artifacts: The Walkers in Skin

This page lists the minor objects of The Walkers in Skin; larger items include [Betsy](betsy.md), the [Plymouth Voyager](plymouth-voyager.md) and the [ground-penetrating radar](ground-penetrating-radar.md).

### Ambushers' shotguns
Guns taken from the roadside ambush in Walkers 08. [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) orders the weapons confiscated, one shotgun goes to [Yusuf](../characters/yusuf-ansari.md), who has said he wants a gun, and another is used to open the lock of the morgue cabinet. See [the roadside ambushers](../characters/minor-walkers.md#roadside-ambushers). *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Ape gold
Gold taken from the party's previous investigation, which Andre describes as one large sock full of gold sitting at the D.C. office. In Walkers 06 the Biz raises it as a way to pay the $10,000 fine offered for the Voyager. See [Jan's Conspiracy](../organizations/jans-conspiracy.md). *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*

### Best of Queen tape
[Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md)'s *Best of Queen* cassette, which comes through the wreck of the Voyager physically intact but plays only static. Queen is also the "music" that Sheriff [McDermish](../characters/sheriff-mcdermish.md) mistakenly reports as a noise complaint in Walkers 03. *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Bone saw
A saw that Kurt fetches from the morgue cabinet at the Roosevelt police station so that [McManley](../characters/donald-mcmanley.md) can carry out an autopsy. See [the Roosevelt police station](../locations/roosevelt-police-station.md). *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Car phone request
A request for a car phone, filed with the Bureau and still pending in Walkers 03. In Walkers 08 the lack of one hurts: with the rental destroyed, the team has no car phone or satellite phone, and their walkie-talkies cannot reach Salt Lake City. *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Cats costumes
School fundraiser costumes that are in the trunk of the loaner squad car [Betsy](betsy.md), and which [Walt](../characters/walt-murphy.md) identifies as *Cats* costumes from Roosevelt's drama department (they "sewed a butthole onto each one"). [Huell](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) buries one to calibrate the ground-penetrating radar, and the costumes are still in the car in Walkers 07 and 08. See [the Roosevelt high school drama department](../organizations/minor-walkers.md#roosevelt-high-school-drama-department). *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md), [Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Ceramic frog
A ceramic frog that hides the keys to the ranch's guest double-wide in Walkers 02. In the cold open of Walkers 06, a ceramic frog hides the spare key to [Doug](../characters/doug.md)'s trailer. *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md), [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*

### Cocaine in shoe heel
[Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md)'s stash, hidden in the heel of a shoe. During the night event it is used as a dental anesthetic for the [Biz](../characters/dick-baxter.md), and Kurt takes some himself. See [the night event](../lore/night-event.md). *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Dead coyote
A coyote shot at the school and kept in Bigelow's emptied porch beer cooler, with ice and bricks on the lid, for [McManley](../characters/donald-mcmanley.md) to examine. [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) finds it clean and groomed, with no ticks or wounds apart from the bullet, and jokes that the Mormons are training coyotes. See [upright coyotes](../monsters/upright-coyotes.md). *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

### Drinking fountain test kits
FBI kits for testing the water from Ballard's fountains. [Gardner](../characters/carl-gardner.md) asks for another kit over the intercom. See [Ballard water contamination](../lore/ballard-water-contamination.md). *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*

### FBI case file
Gardner's copy of the earlier local reports on the ranch, covering lost time, the Ranger's shooting and the mutilations. The party is meant to check it back in, and has not read it yet when it is handed over. See [livestock mutilations](../lore/livestock-mutilations.md). *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*

### French Toast Castle
The Good Hills Diner's eight-inch tower of French toast, held together with toothpicks and drowned in syrup. [Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md) eats only an embarrassingly small portion and is warned it will ruin his day. The leftovers, taken in a to-go box, are used as bait for the goose, who eats them. See [Good Hills Diner](../locations/minor-walkers.md#good-hills-diner) and [Nancy the goose](../characters/nancy-the-goose.md). *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*

### Gold fillings
Replacement fillings for the [Biz](../characters/dick-baxter.md), who loses every filling in the night event. The Roosevelt dentist has only gold on hand, which is non-magnetic. The Biz notes the fit with his nickname and his watch. See [the Roosevelt dentist](../characters/minor-walkers.md#roosevelt-dentist). *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Goosebumps books
The reading of the teenage intruders in the barn: *Welcome to Dead House*, *Monster Blood* and *Stay Out of the Basement*. They belong to [Harrison](../characters/harrison.md). *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*

### Grenade
A grenade produced from [Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md)'s bag in the gym. It kills horses and townsfolk and wounds both Andrew and his double. The duplicate later describes it to the others as "leverage that went off." See [Andrew's bag](what-i-need-bag.md) and [Ballard High School](../locations/ballard-high-school.md). *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

### Hooster's package
A flat-rate package of gadgets that the Biz orders from [Hooster](../characters/hooster.md): a net gun, thermite and a bomb collar, for use against the goose. In Walkers 05 Dante recalls that the order has been placed. *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md), [Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Howard's camera and photographs
[Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md)'s habit of photographing every scene. In Walkers 03 he records the sky, scorch marks, tire tracks, the substation, fences and the community center site. In Walkers 07 the lens is smashed in the gym. In Walkers 08 he photographs the ambush scene, the dead officers and the prisoners, and in Walkers 09 the real Howard takes dozens of blurry Polaroids of the hazmat crews and the black craft. *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md), [Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*

### Howard's Fossil watch
Andrew's 1990s Fossil high-beat mechanical watch, with glow-in-the-dark hands. It keeps working through the dusk event, unlike the battery and digital watches of his companions. See [the dusk event](../lore/dusk-electrical-event.md). *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*

### Howard's jacket
Andrew's jacket, stolen and worn by the goose. [Walt](../characters/walt-murphy.md) says that letting the goose take it "shows weak," and Andrew remarks that she is "in a lovely jacket now." See [Nancy the goose](../characters/nancy-the-goose.md). *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*

### Huell's walkie-talkie
A police-model handset, probably a Motorola, whose proprietary battery has gone missing. In Walkers 09 Andre tells Huell that the battery is missing, and the table discusses where a replacement could be found. *([Walkers 09](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/09.md))*

### Joke pen
A pen from the front desk of the Roosevelt police station that [Huell](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) gives to [Yusuf](../characters/yusuf-ansari.md). *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Kurt's shovel
A shovel that [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) brings along for the exhumation; it was previously used on Chester. See [the exhumation of Shelly Wilmarth](../lore/exhumation-of-shelly-wilmarth.md). *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md))*

### Kurt's stilts
Kurt's Vietnam-era pair, carrying shrapnel, is lost with the Voyager and later recovered from the wreck. A spare pair turns up in the trunk of [Betsy](betsy.md), and the party uses them to open a door and pry at the goose. *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md), [Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Kurt's watch
A small gear-driven battery watch that stops at the dusk event. In Walkers 06 Kurt takes it apart and holds the pieces out to the beam, which sends them rocketing upward. See [the beam](../lore/heat-and-magnet-beam.md). *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*

### LSD tooth
[Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md)'s dental capsule, which shifts his cognition when he bites it during the night event. See [the night event](../lore/night-event.md). *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Mud room key
The key that the naked intruder used on the ranch's back door. [Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md) keeps it, and it suggests that someone holds keys to the estate. See [the naked intruder](../characters/naked-intruder.md). *([Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

### Plastic FBI badge
The Biz's toy badge, which he uses to apply pressure to the people he questions. *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Remington Model 870
The sheriff's Vietnam-era shotgun, which Sheriff [McDermish](../characters/sheriff-mcdermish.md) gives to [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) before fleeing Ballard. It is listed among the party's weapons in Walkers 07. *([Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

### Roosevelt police cruiser
An abandoned cruiser, in better condition than [Betsy](betsy.md), parked in the rocks by the ambush site. It is the source of the spike strip, and the party uses it to carry the ambush bodies. Its radio hears nothing on any preset channel. *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Sheriff's names list
A Post-it note listing absent families and their addresses, which Sheriff [McDermish](../characters/sheriff-mcdermish.md) hands to [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md). See [disappearances in Ballard](../lore/ballard-disappearances.md). *([Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md))*

### Spike strip
A spike strip taken from the roadside ambush. It came from the empty Roosevelt police cruiser parked in the rocks, and Kurt orders it confiscated. *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### The Biz's Casio
The Biz's cheap digital watch, whose seven-segment display is left permanently scrambled by the dusk event. See [the dusk event](../lore/dusk-electrical-event.md). *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md))*
