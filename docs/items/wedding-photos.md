# Wedding Photos

**The Wedding Photos** are a sheet of old wedding photographs, the only thing left in the bedroom safe of the Roulet House, which let the agents identify Margaret Roulet's body by her ring.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Item (photographs) |
| **Holder** | The task force |
| **First appearance** | [Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md) |

## Description

The sheet shows wedding photos that look to be from the 1950s or 1960s, probably taken in Arizona, of a curly-haired woman and a man with close-cropped military hair. Marks in the safe show where other items once sat *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*.

## History

Kurt cracks the safe in the bedroom with an Act Under Pressure roll, forcing it with a fire poker. The poker whips back and cuts his shin for 1 harm, but the safe opens. The photos are the only contents *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*.

In Roulet Estate 02, after Kurt empties his gun into the basement trash pit and the others find a withered, sleeved hand there, Andrew goes to fetch the photos. Consulting them, the agents see that the ring on the hand is the same wedding ring as in the pictures, which Andre confirms identifies the body as that of [Margaret Roulet](../characters/margaret-roulet.md). The agents take the photos with them as evidence when they go for help *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*.
