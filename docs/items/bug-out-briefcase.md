# Bug-out briefcase

**The bug-out briefcase** is an escape kit hidden in a heat vent under [Dick "The Biz" Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md)'s bed, holding cash, Swiss francs, a cheap revolver, a change of clothes and a Swiss passport in the name of "Noah Mueller."

## Description

Kurtis Larson finds the case with a successful Investigate a Mystery roll (11), after searching for false bottoms, loose bricks, floorboards, wallpaper and vents. The heat vent under the Biz's bed had stripped screws. Inside are *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*:

- a few hundred US dollars,
- a much larger sum in Swiss francs,
- a cheap revolver,
- a change of clothes,
- a Swiss passport with the Biz's photo under the name Noah Mueller.

The name Noah Mueller is of uncertain spelling.

## Properties

The kit is not supernatural. It serves as a clue to the Biz's intentions and as a "secret identity" trope: it shows he had a Swiss alias and money ready to disappear.

## History

- **Discovery.** In the early part of the Radford Horrors, [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md) and Huell, joined by Kurt, search the Biz's neglected apartment after he vanishes. The place has dust, cobwebs, a taped-up leather recliner, a heater running, a TV on Animal Planet, rotten fridge meat, and shelves of war memoirs, technical manuals, hard-boiled novels and Russian books. Under a loose floorboard the team finds his share of the gold, about $8,000 to $9,000 in Ziplocs, and cocaine. The Biz had also requested six months of personal leave for a "family emergency," a discount *Playboy* subscription began days before he vanished, and a *Moby-Dick* with its first pages torn out hides a cipher (see [Moby-Dick book cipher](../lore/moby-dick-book-cipher.md)) *([Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md))*.
- **Kurt's reading.** Dante (as Kurt) remarks, "Well, I'm guessing this is his bug-out bag. Totally normal thing to own." Kurt concludes that the Biz had other plans, meant to return, was in touch with a third party and was intercepted, since the Swiss francs were left behind. Asked whether the Biz is a double agent, Kurt answers that "there are degrees of double agent."
- **Aftermath.** The Biz had in fact been taken to Russia, where he is held at the Hotel Metropol and brought before Boris Yeltsin (see [Radford Horrors 01](../episodes/radford-horrors/01.md); [Hotel Metropol](../locations/hotel-metropol.md)). Kurt reads the kit as intended for Switzerland, but the episodes never confirm where the Biz meant to go.

## What remains unknown

- Why the Biz needed an identity as a Swiss citizen, and who he was corresponding with via classified ads in the *Washington City Paper*, the *Washington Informer* and the *Georgetown Current*.
- Whether the Biz ever recovers the kit.
