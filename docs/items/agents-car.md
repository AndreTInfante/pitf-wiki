# The Agents' Car

**The Agents' Car** is the vehicle in which Diane drove the task force to Rhode Island; it loses its battery, is stolen by the Roulet Intruder, and is eventually recovered at a pharmacy parking lot.

## Description

The car carries a car phone and a marked-up map of the area. Diane slept in the back seat under a blanket, and the car is littered with the packages of the Corn Nuts she was made to stop for on the drive *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), [Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*.

## History

**Roulet Estate 01.** The hungover agents find the car in the driveway of the [Roulet House](../locations/roulet-house.md). Andrew first mistakes the blanketed shape in the back seat for a body, but it is Diane. The car will not start and the phone does not work. During the sniper's attack a car window is shot out. Afterward the agents push the car closer to the house, and when they raise the hood they find the battery missing *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*.

**Roulet Estate 02.** While the agents are in the basement, an engine starts outside. They reach the front door in time to see a grey alien driving their car around the corner of the driveway. The monk sprints after it shouting "Pawtucket!" and gives up after about a hundred feet. The players assume the thief put the battery back; the episodes never explain why he removed it. Later a local officer asks for a description of the shooter and the car so that an APB can go out *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*.

**Roulet Estate 03.** The episode opens with a cold open shot from the back seat as the gunman, in a grey alien head and holding a 9mm pistol, drives the car through slush, humming along to "Radar Love" with the fuel gauge in the red. After the agents learn from a young officer that it has been spotted, they find it parked in the lot of a [Brooks pharmacy](../locations/brooks-pharmacy.md) beside a squad car. The gunman's shots into the lot hit the tires of the police car the agents push toward cover; Kurt pries a flat tire off its rim, douses it with vodka from the Biz's flask and sets it alight, adding a bag of stale donuts from the squad car, to make a smoke screen *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*.
