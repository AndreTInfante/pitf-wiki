# Minor items and artifacts: Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable (non-canon one-shot)

!!! note "Non-canon"
    These items appear only in *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, a non-canon one-shot presented as an in-universe anime OVA. Nothing here is part of the main campaign's continuity.

Minor items of the one-shot, in alphabetical order.

### Mirabeth's Business Cards

Cards that [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) presses on other trainers' fighters in the briefing tent as part of her poaching, and on [Addison](../characters/pf-addison.md) at the social. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### Presidential Seal Sticker

A token the [werewolf president](../characters/pf-werewolf-president.md) gives [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) to "show that you met me," after praising his flag and saying privately that turning into a dog is pretty cool. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### The $100 Bill and the $2

The cash for Derk's hot dogs at [Miguel](../characters/pf-miguel.md)'s stand. Mirabeth hands Derk the money so that he can pay, and he hands over $100 for hot dogs that cost a dollar each. The honest vendor returns 98 hot dogs plus $2. The $2 later pays for the video call Derk makes to Mirabeth at 2 a.m. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### The Bent Quarter

A roped-area guard asks [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) to show his kung fu and hands him a quarter. Derk bends it between two fingers ("Iron fist"), putting a ninety-degree twist in it. He still has the coin at the end of the one-shot, having straightened it out. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### Video Phones

Street-corner video phones with a map application that shows satellite photography. This world has no cell phones, so [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) uses one to call her bridge-club friend, and she has an in-home one on which she takes Derk's late-night call. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
