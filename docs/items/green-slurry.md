# Green Slurry

**Green Slurry** is a homemade, highly concentrated peyote preparation, kept in a jar with an eyedropper in the hidden attic lair of the Roulet House.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Item (drug) |
| **Aliases** | peyote slurry |
| **Holder** | The task force (taken from the lair) |
| **First appearance** | [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md) |

## Description

The slurry is in a homemade-looking jar with a lid in the [hidden attic lair](../locations/hidden-attic-lair.md), alongside a cooler holding [beta blockers](beta-blockers.md). An eyedropper with it suggests to Kurt that the occupant has been gradually taking some out to use *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*.

## Properties

Kurt cracks the jar and sniffs it. The smell is oddly familiar; the walls seem to close in and something moves at the edge of his vision. A Sharp roll (5 + 3 = 8) is not enough to identify the mixture exactly, but he can tell that it is very concentrated and probably contains peyote. He hands his sidearm to Andrew until he has caught his breath *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*.

The house's water also gives the agents hallucinations (see [The Drugged Water](../lore/drugged-water.md)), but no one in the episodes connects it to the slurry *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*.

## History

Kurt and Andrew bring the jar down the chimney with the looted guns, money and valuables, so that the intruder has fewer resources if he returns. Kurt plans to have it analyzed. Its purpose, and whether the Roulets' "visitations" were drug trips, are never established *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*.
