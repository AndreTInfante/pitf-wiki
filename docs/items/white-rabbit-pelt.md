# White Rabbit Pelt

The **White Rabbit Pelt** is the gutted skin of a white rabbit that [Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) retrieved from a treetop near the spot where the [Circle Chasers](../organizations/circle-chasers.md) filmed a rabbit being snatched.

## Description

The pelt is a white rabbit's skin with stringy meat and blood stuck to the inside and all of the internal organs gone. On a superficial inspection it looks ripped open rather than cut or chewed *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

## Properties

The pelt answered the team's question "Where did it go?" It had been carried up a tree about 100 feet from the snatch, and Andre tells the players that the creature is "very comfortable moving vertically". [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md) pointed out that [Gene's shoe](genes-shoe.md), found in another tree, might likewise have been hung there after the incident rather than during it, and suggested the creature may return to hang its prey. Andrew's Rita-voiced verdict was that it could still be a werewolf *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

## History

In [Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md) the Circle Chasers show the team a three-second VHS clip in which a flashlight darts across a trail and a rabbit is taken. The team goes to the rabbit site (see [Woods Trail and Rabbit Site](../locations/woods-trail-and-rabbit-site.md)) to look for a blood trail. After about half an hour of searching, blood drips onto Andrew from a tree about 100 feet from where the rabbit was taken.

Zack volunteers Huell to climb. Andrew's Help Out gives Huell a +1 but exposes Andrew to danger. Huell slips, catches himself on the branch below and is racked under both arms, taking 1 harm and a cylindrical bruise along each arm. He comes down with the pelt. Kurt suggests bagging it for [Donald McManley](../characters/donald-mcmanley.md), and the team marks the map with Comstock's ridge, the road spot and the rabbit site to start building a range. That night the pelt goes into the hotel mini fridge so it will not decompose *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

In [Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md), when Kurt compares the map against the tunnel exit and the Spud Hut distribution center, Andre says "the rabbit thing" is nowhere near either. The pelt is not mentioned again.
