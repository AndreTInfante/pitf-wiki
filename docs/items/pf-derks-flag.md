# Derk's Flag

!!! note "Non-canon"
    Derk's Flag belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Derk's Flag** is the cape-sized butcher-paper flag that [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) draws on the floor of the [Public Library](../locations/pf-public-library.md) for [the Flag Contest](../lore/pf-flag-contest.md).

|---|---|

## Description

A tattered butcher-paper flag, large enough to be worn as a cape, marked with bugs and wet handprints and knee prints. It shows scorpions (the president reads them as "giant scorpions attacking the earth"), the stars as seen from space, a corgi-like dog that is the president and, added later, hot dogs in taco shells. The taco dogs are better drawn than anything else on it, but the composition is incoherent, and laying the colors over the black of space makes them look odd. Derk says he should have drawn only hot dogs. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

Derk draws it over about an hour and a half in the library's children's room (a Superior roll of 6) while parked among toddlers. [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) tears it off the floor as she rescues him. Outside the community center she shows it to Derk as his art and has him draw something smaller. He never draws a better flag, and he simply adds the taco dogs to the same one. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

At the [Presidential Event Hall](../locations/pf-presidential-event-hall.md), Derk presents it to the [werewolf president](../characters/pf-werewolf-president.md), who praises the hot dog as a patriotic food and the taco shell as "multiculturalism," learns that the dog is himself, and promises "real serious consideration." He gives Derk a presidential-seal sticker to "show that you met me." During the fight, Derk stands up for the president against [Ovain](../characters/pf-ovain.md) with "Hey, be nice to him! He liked my flag!" *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
