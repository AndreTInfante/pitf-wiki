# Philosopher's Stone

!!! note "Non-canon"
    The Philosopher's Stone belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

The **Philosopher's Stone** is the grand prize of the [World Martial Arts Tournament](../organizations/pf-world-martial-arts-tournament.md) and the reason [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) enters.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Item |
| **Holder** | The tournament, as its prize |
| **First appearance** | [Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md) |

## Description

The stone never appears in the one-shot; it is described only as the tournament's grand prize. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Properties

The winner gets access to it for a couple of minutes. It is usually used in a high-efficiency hospital setting, because tournament fighters are very death- and injury-prone. Andre tells Dante that it can be used to resurrect Fatty Gibbs. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

Derk's friend [Fatty Gibbs](../characters/pf-fatty-gibbs.md), a sweet sumo wrestler, died of a coronary artery problem earlier last year. Derk wants the stone to bring him back, and that is why he agreed to enter. The tournament is suspended after a doping scandal and may be canceled, so the prize is out of reach as the one-shot ends, and Derk is lost in [Wisukonshin](../locations/pf-wisukonshin.md). *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
