# Utah cleanup crew

The **Utah cleanup crew** is a group of three skinwalkers in gray maintenance uniforms, armed and carrying plastic sheeting and bungee cords, whom the team confronts outside the Roosevelt police station.

## Overview

While the team was inside the station, they saw three men in gray maintenance uniforms looking over the team's cars in the parking lot. The men claimed they needed the substation keys kept at the station. When told they would be searched, one drew a gun *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*. Their utility bags held guns, plastic sheeting and bungee cords, like the sheet plastic on the bodies in the gym ceiling at [Ballard High School](../locations/ballard-high-school.md), which the team read as a cleanup crew.

## History

### The firefight (Walkers 08)

Kurt drew fast enough that the gunman's shot went wide, and shot him through the arm. The replica Andrew, played by Rita, rolled a 3, panicked, shattered a window of [Betsy](../items/betsy.md) with a stray round, and was hit in the shoulder (see [Andrew Howard (duplicate)](../characters/andrew-howard-duplicate.md)). The gunman was shot dead with a head wound that McManley later swabbed and the team credited to the Biz, while the other two ran. Kurt rammed them with Betsy against the wrought-iron parking-lot gates and restrained them. Both stayed silent, and Andre said that "the facade of acting like a person has just completely dropped away" *([Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*.

[Donald McManley](../characters/donald-mcmanley.md) examined the survivors, who had a slightly high body temperature, irregular pupils and bad teeth. The dead man's autopsy showed no stomach, about eight feet of intestine, a small fatty liver and green fluid in the head wound, and his skull held a non-human brain-like organ; see [Pilot and host biology](../lore/pilot-and-host-biology.md) and [Pilot organ in Tupperware](../items/pilot-organ.md). Later the two prisoners were slumped and empty-headed, and a flashlight through an eye showed light at the back of the mouth: their pilots had fled into the drop ceiling.
