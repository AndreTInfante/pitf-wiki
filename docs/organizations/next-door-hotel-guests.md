# Next-door Hotel Guests

The **Next-door Hotel Guests** are a group of secretive occupants of a room at the Smith Hotel in Dahlonega, the room the Biz bugged in [Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md).

## Overview

The group's room was booked under one name, occupied by someone else, and paid for with a card in a third name. The [desk clerk](../characters/smith-hotel-desk-clerk.md) told the party that scary men came and went and that one of them had offered him money to keep quiet *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md), [Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md)).*

## Members

- The booker (unseen): an armed, rude, short-haired, military-looking man who sounded Midwestern and paid the clerk $100 for silence. When the clerk described him, the Biz said "That is him" *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.
- A curly-haired blonde woman in a bathrobe, who asked a battered [Andrew Howard](../characters/andrew-howard.md) "Are you okay, honey?" in the hall *([Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md))*.
- A suited man with a military haircut, who whispered to the woman, steered her back inside and glared at Andrew.
- A laughing man in a big cowboy hat and dark sunglasses, whom [Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) glimpsed entering the room and found oddly familiar for a split second. He is never identified on screen.

## History

In Appalachia 02 the clerk reported the room to [Dick "The Biz" Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md) and lent him a master key. The Biz entered room 111 alone, found a delivered basket of wine and chocolates and a room that looked staged for a sexual encounter, and hid a motion-sensing [spy camera and microphone](../items/spy-camera-and-microphone.md) there, the camera beside the VCR and the microphone in the bedside drawer. He suspected a setup, possibly involving kids *([Appalachia 02](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/02.md))*.

The guests crossed paths with the party in the hall in Appalachia 03, after Andrew returned to the hotel by cab *([Appalachia 03](../episodes/things-in-appalachia/03.md))*.

In the epilogue in [Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md), the public story is that FBI agents were caught surveilling Governor Clinton in a Georgia hotel room, and a talk-show host blames Jan Martense for it. The Biz, interviewed, says he thought he had been spying on a prostitute and had not known Clinton was in town. The episodes never state outright that the guests were Clinton's party, but the sequence points that way. See the [Clinton surveillance scandal](../lore/clinton-surveillance-scandal.md).
