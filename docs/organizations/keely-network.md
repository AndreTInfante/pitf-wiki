# Keely Network

**The Keely Network** is a community of UFO enthusiasts who trade messages on electronic message boards, and the source of the letter that sent the task force to the Roulet house.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Online community (message boards) |
| **Members** | An unnamed correspondent who knew Andrew Howard; a friend of Margaret Roulet |
| **First appearance** | [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md) |

## Overview

In the recap that opens [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md), Andre explains the origin of the trip: someone from the Keely Network message boards who knew Andrew wrote to him that their friend [Margaret Roulet](../characters/margaret-roulet.md) had been experiencing "visitations" and then vanished. *([Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*

The letter reached Andrew on Christmas Eve, when Big Bill handed over a padded envelope that an FBI courier had asked him to drop off. It had no return address and held a second envelope wrapped in aluminum foil, which contained the letter and a hand-drawn pencil sketch of some trees (see [Foil-Wrapped Letter](../items/foil-wrapped-letter.md)). Andre later says that the black triangles came from the sketch. The correspondent is never named. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md), [Roulet Estate 02](../episodes/roulet-estate/02.md))*
