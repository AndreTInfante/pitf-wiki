# Minor organizations: The Roulet Estate

Minor organizations of The Roulet Estate, in alphabetical order.

### LTTE

The Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam, the Sri Lankan insurgent group that [Kurt Larson](../characters/kurt-larson.md) says he was sent to fight in early 1993. He mentions it in passing in the epilogue, where he says he postponed the assassination of [Ranasinghe Premadasa](../characters/minor-roulet.md#ranasinghe-premadasa) before being called back after Waco. The group never appears. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*

### Perot's Insurgency Task Force

The new unit that President-elect [Ross Perot](../characters/ross-perot.md) is forming to investigate the "insurgency incidents." On Christmas Eve 1992 [Big Bill](../characters/big-bill.md) tells the agents that Perot believes their task force is a cover for [George H. W. Bush](../characters/george-h-w-bush.md) weaponizing the FBI against his political enemies. Perot has asked him to resign and will disband and reassign the [Paranatural Incident Task Force](paranatural-incident-task-force.md) on Inauguration Day. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))*

### Salvation Army

The charity for which the teenage carolers collect at the task force office on Christmas Eve 1992. [Huell Kendrick](../characters/huell-kendrick.md) gives the carolers his remaining cigarettes and the Biz gives one of his gold nuggets. *([Roulet Estate 01](../episodes/roulet-estate/01.md))* See also [Salvation Army Carolers](../characters/minor-roulet.md#salvation-army-carolers).

### The Humanists

The group about which [Boris Yeltsin](../characters/boris-yeltsin.md) questions the Biz. After offering him a Cuban cigar in the former Soviet Parliament building, Yeltsin says, "Tell me everything you know about the humanists." Nothing more is revealed about who they are. *([Roulet Estate 03](../episodes/roulet-estate/03.md))*
