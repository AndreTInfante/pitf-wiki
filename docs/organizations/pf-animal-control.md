# Animal Control

!!! note "Non-canon"
    Animal Control belongs to the non-canon one-shot *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, not to the main campaign.

**Animal Control** is the agency the library calls about a "shaved baboon," which is [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md). *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## Overview

Library staff call both child services and Animal Control "just to cover our bases" after Derk eats just under half the snack table in the children's room of the [Public Library](../locations/pf-public-library.md), among crying toddlers. The responders arrive saying they "got a call about some kind of shaved baboon" and ask someone to point them to the baboon. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

## History

[Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) sends Derk off along the emergency-exit signs, tears the butcher-paper drawing off the floor and walks past Animal Control on her way out. During her debriefing, a grizzled detective asks whether she had anything to do with "an animal control call to a public library that ended in a riot." He says Animal Control "was sick of prank calls," and Mirabeth observes that there was no animal there. The riot is never explained. *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
