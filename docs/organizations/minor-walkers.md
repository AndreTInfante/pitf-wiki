# Minor organizations: The Walkers in Skin

This page lists the minor businesses, institutions and communities that appear in The Walkers in Skin; larger groups include [Bigelow's research operation](bigelows-research-operation.md) and the [skinwalker faction](skinwalker-faction.md).

### Ballard Sheriff's Office
The law enforcement of Ballard, Utah, which amounts to one man, Sheriff [McDermish](../characters/sheriff-mcdermish.md), working from a double-wide trailer that is also his home. In Walkers 03 he serves sweet tea at a card table with a Monopoly game underway. In Walkers 06 he reports that over half the high school is absent, that houses have been empty for days and that the diner is nearly empty, and he then arms [Kurt](../characters/kurt-larson.md) and flees the state with his son. By Walkers 07 and Walkers 08 the department is empty, and the party only mentions that the sheriff left town. See [the sheriff's double-wide](../locations/minor-walkers.md#sheriffs-double-wide) and [disappearances in Ballard](../lore/ballard-disappearances.md). *([Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md), [Walkers 08](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/08.md))*

### Budget Suites of America
A hotel chain owned by [Bigelow](../characters/robert-bigelow.md) and recommended by [Gardner](../characters/carl-gardner.md), who promises kitchens and no bed bugs. Kurt asks whether dentists are staying there, a nod to the table's running gag about dentists' conferences bumping the team from hotels. *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*

### Dollar Tree
A rival discount store in Roosevelt, loathed by the cashier at Stewart's, who thanks the party for not patronizing the "interloper" and complains that "they don't pay copyright on the songs." See [Stewart's](../locations/minor-walkers.md#stewarts). *([Walkers 05](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/05.md))*

### Hertz Rent-A-Car
The rental company that supplied the party's Plymouth Voyager. After the van vanishes and reappears, a Hertz clerk pages [Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md) to report it blocking East Lagoon Street in Roosevelt. See [the Plymouth Voyager](../items/plymouth-voyager.md) and [the vanishing car](../lore/vanishing-car-incident.md). *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*

### Mormon community of Ballard
The religious majority in Ballard and the surrounding area, and the group the party first suspects. The community's church holds the grave of Shelly Wilmarth, and in Walkers 01 [Alberta](../characters/alberta-wilmarth.md) asks the FBI to force it to exhume her mother. Sheriff [McDermish](../characters/sheriff-mcdermish.md) complains about "nosy Mormons," the bartender suspects [Andrew](../characters/andrew-howard.md) of being a "Mormon fed," and in Walkers 06 the sheriff notes that most of the missing townspeople are Mormon. After the gym fight, the duplicate of Andrew guesses "Mormons or a religious cult thing," and the players joke about Mormons training coyotes. See [the exhumation](../lore/exhumation-of-shelly-wilmarth.md). *([Walkers 01](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/01.md), [Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md), [Walkers 06](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/06.md), [Walkers 07](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/07.md))*

### Roosevelt high school drama department
The source of the *Cats* fundraiser costumes that the party finds in the trunk of the loaner squad car [Betsy](../items/betsy.md). [Walt](../characters/walt-murphy.md) identifies them as *Cats* costumes and credits the Roosevelt drama teacher, described as a "big musical theater lady." See [Cats costumes](../items/minor-walkers.md#cats-costumes). *([Walkers 04](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/04.md))*
