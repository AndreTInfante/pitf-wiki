# Minor organizations: Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable (non-canon one-shot)

!!! note "Non-canon"
    These groups appear only in *Philosopher's Fist 3.J/2: Unchainable*, a non-canon one-shot presented as an in-universe anime OVA. Nothing here is part of the main campaign's continuity.

Minor groups of the one-shot who do not have their own pages, in alphabetical order. The larger groups have pages of their own: the [World Martial Arts Tournament](pf-world-martial-arts-tournament.md), the [Secret Service](pf-secret-service.md), [The Alien Faction](pf-alien-faction.md), [Pure Human Martial Artists](pf-pure-human-martial-artists.md) and [Animal Control](pf-animal-control.md).

### Corporate Sponsors

Almost everyone at the [World Martial Arts Tournament](pf-world-martial-arts-tournament.md) wears sponsor branding. Sponsors fund fighters in return for a share of the prize pool, and most fighters live on fixed incomes from them. [Mirabeth](../characters/pf-mirabeth.md) and [Derk](../characters/pf-derk.md) did not submit their paperwork in time, though a dog-food company had shown interest. After the Ovain fight, Colonel Sparago tells Mirabeth that Derk "probably needs to be sponsored." *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*

### Wizards

A medium-sized group of entrants at the safety briefing, "basically wizards," who specialize in psychic or magical attacks. They study the core magic curriculum some eighteen hours a day in their formative years, so they are dim and barely literate outside it, and as a clan they have a terrible reputation. They are one of the three notable groups Mirabeth considers eavesdropping on, along with the [Alien Faction](pf-alien-faction.md) and the [Pure Human Martial Artists](pf-pure-human-martial-artists.md). *([Philosopher's Fist](../episodes/philosophers-fist/index.md))*
