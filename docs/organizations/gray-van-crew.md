# Gray van crew

**The gray van crew** is a five-man paramilitary-style team that drives gray vans around Radford, abducts people, scrubs crime scenes of evidence, and bags the creature in the Douglas Pinball Emporium. Their employer is unknown.

## Overview

The crew first appears as the unseen force behind the abduction of the arcade's overnight worker [Elizabeth George](../characters/elizabeth-george.md). The cold open of [Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md) shows muffled thrashing, the sound of a Taser, a woman's limp arm and a man in a black balaclava carrying her off. When the team investigates the [Douglas Pinball Emporium](../locations/douglas-pinball-emporium.md), they find a broken Taser tine with blood, a smear of blood on a machine corner, a missing first-aid kit and hand towels, and signs of recent cleaning. The security tape has been swapped, and the back fence has been cut and repaired *([Radford Horrors 03](../episodes/radford-horrors/03.md))*. The kidnappers are evidently professionals.

## The diner footage

In [Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md) the waitress at the [Lonely Mountain Diner](../locations/lonely-mountain-diner.md) gets her camera working, and its footage shows the raid on the emporium across the street at about four or five in the morning. A gray van pulls up and five men hop out: one with long brown hair in a ponytail, the others with military haircuts, several with their faces covered. A shadow that moves wrongly shows in the emporium window. One man opens the lock with what could be a bump key, there is a scuffle inside, and a few minutes later three of the men carry two bags to the van. The others follow with equipment, and the van leaves *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*.

Kurt's Investigate a Mystery roll reads this as an organized cleanup team, more paramilitary than military. The ponytailed man moves like an action movie rather than a real combat veteran, and Andre likens him to a LARPer. The shadow in the window was not human and seemed to be trying to get out as the door opened *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*. It is the [emporium intruder](../monsters/emporium-intruder.md).

## Earlier activity

- The waitress says she has seen the gray vans for a couple of months, around the time of the "right to roam" dispute over the woods, and that several vans were also parked around the [Ferry Lodge](../locations/ferry-lodge.md) during the IRS raid. Kurt wonders whether they are associated with the Safe Environment Fund *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*.
- Harold, the Blockbuster clerk, saw a homeless man dragged into a van by a man with a ponytail a month and a half to two months earlier; the man later reappeared. Andre confirms that this matches [Billy Thornton](../characters/billy-thornton.md), who had complained to the team about being abducted *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*.
- Sheriff [Molly Underhill](../characters/molly-underhill.md) has seen gray vans around town and assumed they belonged to the construction work *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*.

## Unresolved

The employer is never established. The team suspects the Safe Environment Fund because the vans appeared when it did, and Daniel Webster passes that suspicion to FBI Director [Rudy Giuliani](../characters/rudy-giuliani.md), who says he will put a researcher on it *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*. It is not confirmed on screen that the same crew took Elizabeth George.

## Trivia

- Ponytails become a running gag after Billy Thornton's story of being "kidnapped by chimps and molested by John Travolta." It culminates in Zack asking, "Does she have a ponytail?" about a customer Harold describes *([Radford Horrors 04](../episodes/radford-horrors/04.md))*.
