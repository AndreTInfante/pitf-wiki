# FBI Salt Lake City field office

The **FBI Salt Lake City field office** is the medium-sized local Bureau office where the task force picks up the case file on Sherman Ranch before heading to Ballard.

## Overview

The office is medium-sized compared with the one in D.C. [Big Bill](../characters/big-bill.md) set one condition for the Utah trip: stop here for case files on earlier reports from the area, and work with the local FBI *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*. The team flew in, picked up a rented 1990 Plymouth Voyager at the airport and drove to the office; the field office itself lent them nothing. A secretary confirmed that Big Bill had phoned ahead and sent them to a second-floor conference room. See [Plymouth Voyager](../items/plymouth-voyager.md).

## Members

- **[Carl Gardner](../characters/carl-gardner.md).** Short, heavy and hearty, with a big personality. He thumps shoulders, calls people "son", prefers Leno to Letterman, dislikes Clinton, and recognized [Dick Baxter](../characters/dick-baxter.md) as "the fella who spied on Clinton". He had heard of Kurt's CIA branch, [UTAW](utaw.md), as the "explode the cigars" outfit.
- **Philip Bishop.** Gardner's tall, thin, silent partner. He stiffened at Huell's "So, Mormons, am I right?" and four minutes of sweaty silence followed.
- **Shelly.** Staff voice on the intercom, called when Gardner needs another drinking-fountain test kit.

## History

### The briefing (Walkers 02)

Gardner's rundown covered four earlier reports: a Native American former Army Ranger on the ranch who said he shot a snooper by the barn (no physical evidence), a young woman who saw her dead mother (they checked for twins), cattle mutilation blamed on lightning, and informal reports of lights and lost time that Gardner put down to drinking *([Walkers 02](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/02.md))*. He added that "the billionaire" tenant "skeeves me the hell out", and after stepping out to find the name said it was Robert Bigelow, a real estate baron whose security man had fired the shot. He gave the team a copy of the case file, to be checked back in when they leave.

The conference-room corkboard of yarn and Post-its was not about the ranch. It concerned a separate local case, "a drinking fountain pervert", over live sperm in public water. Gardner offered tips: Bigelow's Budget Suites of America hotels, a "friend of Bonaparte" discount at a nearby gas station, and Utah food such as funeral potatoes, dirty soda and Jell-O salad. See [Ballard water contamination](../lore/ballard-water-contamination.md).

### Later mentions (Walkers 03)

In [Walkers 03](../episodes/walkers-in-skin/03.md) Asked whether the task force has "men on" the town's water problem, Dante recalls that the FBI does: "we talked to the guys that were investigating; that was their little corkboard with the thread and whatnot."
