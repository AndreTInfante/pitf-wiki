    The Summer Service¶

    Page
    Amos Shaw Amos Shaw is the unnaturally fit preacher of Marblehead's ocean-obsessed church, who serves fishy communion wine and owns the blood-soaked house the team finds in The Summer Service.
    Arnold Weathers Arnold Weathers is a wealthy Marblehead selectman who is kept chained inside his island house, a bloated worm host found in a tub of black fluid and destroyed by Huell and Andrew.
    Briggs Briggs is a drunk, one-footed fisherman and veteran who lives on a barely floating boat at the old docks of Marblehead, Massachusetts, and the first local to give the task force an outsider's view of the town's church.
    Carter Carter is Kurt Larson's former CIA fixer turned overseas arms dealer, whose life Kurt once saved and who supplies the deniable M28 Davy Crockett as a final favor.
    Chief of Police The Chief of Police is the hostile, bloated head of the Marblehead police and a member of the Marblehead Board of Selectmen.
    Douglas Scott Douglas Scott is the big, gentle, lumberjack-looking father of Lena Scott and husband of Irene Scott, a Marblehead local who hoped the missing scientists would call back.
    Esteban Esteban is a McDonald's worker in Marblehead whose clothes smell of beans, a problem the team traces to his church-going uncle.
    Gray-skinned dream visitor The gray-skinned dream visitor is a nude, dead, deformed gray man who appears in Andrew Howard's sleep-paralysis dream to warn him away from the water.
    Henry Henry is Donald McManley's "useless" assistant, sent to Salem by Vespa to fetch ivermectin, who calls in with a gore-covered umbrella having "killed something."
    Horace Cromwell Horace Cromwell is the banker on the Marblehead Board of Selectmen, a silent, bloated and visibly tense man at the back of the selectmen's meeting in The Summer Service.
    Irene Scott Irene Scott is the chatty, friendly mother of Lena Scott, who runs Scott Insurance from the family home in Marblehead.
    Jester Jester is the devout desk clerk of the team's hotel in Marblehead, a church member and selectman who owns a bag of crystal marbles and feeds Arnold, and who ends infected and killed while driving the stolen FBI car.
    Lena Scott Lena Scott is a friendly six-year-old in Marblehead who hugs strangers and fell ill on the beach the day after the offshore explosion.
    Ma Gramoire Ma Gramoire is a tiny, blunt, absinthe-drinking old woman of Marblehead, poisoned by the sea, the last known contact of the missing scientists, who later sends Madeline Weathers to the team.
    Madeline Weathers Madeline Weathers is the niece of the missing selectman Arnold Weathers, a Beetle-driving young woman who comes to the task force for help in the storm-lashed Marblehead of The Summer Service and ends the case vowing...
    The Mayor of Marblehead The Mayor of Marblehead is the unnamed, bloated and near-translucent head of the town's Board of Selectmen, who also preaches the sermons in the Sons of Adam church in place of Amos Shaw.
    Older Guard officer The older Guard officer is an unnamed National Guard officer in command of the checkpoint at the entrance to Marblehead, who debriefs the task force at the end of The Summer Service.
    The radio contact The radio contact is an unnamed, cheerful voice on the task force's pocket radio who supplies the anti-worm agent, tracks the angel thing and plans the nuclear shot with Kurt in the finale of The Summer Service.
    Ram and Arms innkeeper The Ram and Arms innkeeper is the very old, cheerful and formal keeper of the Ram and Arms Inn in Marblehead, who checks the task force in at the very end of The Radford Horror and gives them the Sons of Adam Bible.

    Derek Edwards Derek Edwards is a tall, bearded amateur photographer and contractor for Auraria's small newspaper, the Gold Bug, whose Chewbacca costume was stolen from Apollo's dry cleaners and later turned up on a dead man.
    Eddie Jones Eddie Jones is a member of the Circle Chasers, the teenage Bigfoot hunters of Dahlonega, Georgia, who holds the theory that Bigfoot is a population of feral humans and who asks Kurt what life in the CIA is like.
